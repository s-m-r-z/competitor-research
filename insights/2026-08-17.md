## Regulatory: California DROP — Ongoing Compliance Activity
- August 1 enforcement is now 17 days in. Law firms (Alston & Bird, Fenwick, Freshfields, TrustArc) continue publishing compliance guidance, indicating sustained corporate demand for interpretation of the $200/day/request penalty structure.
- Over 600 registered data brokers must now access DROP at least every 45 days and delete matching consumer records.
- **Market implication:** The free government DROP tool increasingly competes with paid services for California residents. Services without clear differentiation beyond California compliance are at greatest risk. Non-public broker coverage (OneRep Pro, Privacy Bee), speed of removal (Incogni), and human oversight (DeleteMe, Kanary) are emerging as the axes on which differentiation will be argued.

## Market Summary
Privacy Bee is dominating the news cycle heading into late August — its PCMag perfect score and EraseMe.app acquisition within a single week put it in an unmatched earned-media position, while competitors remain quiet or rely on existing promotional momentum. The California DROP platform's enforcement reality continues to pressure the entire market to articulate value beyond what the free state tool now offers California residents.

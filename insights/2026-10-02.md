## Regulatory & Market

- **Connecticut data broker law** went live October 1, 2026 (yesterday): data brokers must register with CT Dept. of Consumer Protection and comply with deletion requests via forthcoming universal mechanism — adds compliance burden on brokers, potential tailwind for removal services in CT
- **California DROP** (launched Jan 2026, enforcement since Aug 1, 2026): all 500+ registered CA brokers must check DROP every 45 days and process verified deletion requests — free government alternative continues to pressure paid services for CA-based consumers
- Over 20 US states now have comprehensive privacy laws with opt-out rights; state-by-state legislative expansion remains a double-edged sword (more consumer awareness = more potential customers, but also more free/government alternatives)

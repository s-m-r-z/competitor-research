# Competitor Insights — 2026-08-22

## DeleteMe
- Priced at $129/year per person; family plans available; covers approximately 750 data brokers
- Uses a manual, risk-prioritized removal approach with re-checks every 90 days
- No free tier, leaving it vulnerable to Optery's free offering on the low end
- Ranked third overall in recent 2026 roundups (behind Incogni and Aura)

## Privacy Bee
- Transitioned from a single flat price to a three-tier structure: Essentials, Pro, and Signature
- Now offers monthly billing options, multi-year discounts, and multi-person coverage
- Differentiates with an aggressive removal approach: uses legal threats, regulatory complaints, and multi-channel pressure when brokers ignore standard opt-outs

## Kanary
- Rebranded and relaunched as **Kanary Copilot** mobile app (released October 2025 on iOS; Android waitlist)
- Gamifies privacy tasks, presenting opt-out actions as completable cards (~10 min each) during downtime
- Covers 300+ data broker sites; targets privacy-conscious mobile-first users

## Removaly
- **Shut down** — service is no longer operational; customers actively searching for alternatives
- Creates an opening for competitors to target Removaly's former customer base with migration offers

## OneRep
- Ongoing trust concerns surfacing in press: founder holds an ownership stake in Nuwber, one of the data broker sites the service claims to remove you from
- Multiple independent reviewers flagging the conflict of interest, which is generating negative sentiment and may be driving customer churn

## Incogni
- Added new **Protect plan** at $20.74/month bundling the Unlimited data removal plan with Coveron (NordVPN's identity protection service)
- **Custom Removals** feature (manual specialist-assisted removal from sites outside the standard 420+ broker list) now available on Unlimited, Family Unlimited, and Protect plans
- Standard plan starts at $7.49/month (annual); Unlimited at $17.99/month (annual); Family Standard ~$3.99/person/month
- Ranked **#1 overall** in multiple 2026 roundups; the Protect bundle positions it as an all-in-one privacy suite

## Optery
- Holds **CNET Editors' Choice** and **PCMag Editors' Choice** (every year 2022–2026); ranked #1 by Consumer Reports in recent independent study
- Four tiers as of July 2026: Free Basic (quarterly exposure reports), Core ($39/yr, 380+ sites), Extended ($149/yr, 555+ sites), Ultimate ($249/yr, 635+ sites)
- Only service with a genuinely free tier and international coverage (US, Canada, AU, NZ, South Africa)
- Monthly scan/removal/reporting cadence across all paid tiers

## Regulatory & Market Context
- **California DROP Platform** — August 1, 2026 enforcement deadline is now active: 600+ registered data brokers must retrieve and process deletion requests at least every 45 days or face **$200/day/consumer fines** (theoretical $1.5B liability per missed cycle)
- 300,000+ California residents have already submitted DROP deletion requests
- 20+ US states now have comprehensive privacy laws giving consumers opt-out and deletion rights
- The DROP mandate creates strong tailwinds for opt-out services, as consumers are increasingly aware they can act on their privacy rights

## Market Summary
The data broker opt-out market is consolidating around a few clear winners — Incogni leads on value and feature breadth with its new Protect bundle, while Optery holds editorial top-spot and press credibility; Removaly's shutdown creates a near-term acquisition opportunity for any competitor with an active marketing campaign. California's DROP enforcement going live August 1 is the biggest regulatory catalyst of the year, elevating consumer awareness industry-wide.

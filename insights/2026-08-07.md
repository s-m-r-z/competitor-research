# Competitor Insights — 2026-08-07

## Regulatory Environment (Critical — Affects All Players)
- **CPPA board meeting concluded today (August 6–7, 2026)** — agenda included Data Broker Regulation Amendments and Compliance Audits; no public board decisions announced yet, but outcomes are expected to shape DROP enforcement posture for Q3 2026
- **California DROP enforcement is now in day 7** — 322,000+ registered Californians have queued deletion requests; 600+ registered brokers required to access the platform every 45 days and process verified deletions within 45 days; $200/day fines apply with no cure period
- **CalPrivacy Data Broker Strike Force** is actively investigating compliance; the agency's first DELETE Act enforcement action (January 2026, $45,000 fine against Datamasters/Rickenbacher Data LLC) signals willingness to levy financial penalties — more actions expected as the August 1 processing deadline passes
- California's DROP precedent is being watched by other states; New Jersey's data broker law (enacted June 30, 2026) targets both brokers and upstream data suppliers — widening the regulatory risk landscape

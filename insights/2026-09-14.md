The dominant story this week is the **Surfshark-Optery acquisition** (announced September 7, 2026), which deepens industry consolidation and gives Surfshark Group control of two leading consumer removal services (Optery + Incogni) alongside its enterprise product Ironwall — increasing competitive pressure on independent players like DeleteMe, Kanary, and Privacy Bee.

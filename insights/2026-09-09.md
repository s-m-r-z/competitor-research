# Competitor Insights — 2026-09-09

## Privacy Bee
- **PCMag 5-Star Rating (August 2026):** Privacy Bee became the first and only data removal service to earn a perfect 5/5 rating and Editors' Choice from PCMag—a milestone across 44 years of the publication's independent testing. Coverage cited at 1,128 data brokers and removals from 185,000+ custom sites.
- **EraseMe.app Acquisition (August 12, 2026):** Privacy Bee acquired EraseMe.app, absorbing its customer base and proprietary removal technology. EraseMe.app users are being migrated to Privacy Bee's platform; new visitors to EraseMe.app are redirected to Privacy Bee. This consolidation expands Privacy Bee's tech stack for exposure identification, removal management, and monitoring.
- **September 2026 Positioning:** Between the PCMag accolade and the acquisition, Privacy Bee is mounting a strong marketing push heading into Q4. This is the most significant competitive development in the space in recent weeks.

## DeleteMe
- Remains a widely recommended mid-tier service for users outside California, with data removed from ~122 sites on a 90-day re-check cycle, starting at ~$10.75/month.
- A Consumer Reports investigation (2024, still widely cited) rated DeleteMe as a "mid-level performer," trailing Optery and EasyOptOuts on removal rate. No major product announcements identified in the last 48 hours.
- Regulatory context: DeleteMe's value proposition is strengthened for non-California users, as California's DROP platform now provides a free alternative for CA residents.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- **California DROP fully operational (August 1, 2026):** Data brokers must now access California's Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform at least every 45 days to process consumer deletion requests. Non-compliance penalties reach $200/day. This shifts some competitive pressure—California residents now have a free government alternative for batch opt-outs, potentially reducing demand for paid services among that segment.
- **JEC Senate Report (February 2026):** A Joint Economic Committee report on data brokers continues to fuel legislative interest at the federal level, sustaining public awareness and press coverage that benefits the whole category.
- **New entrant tracking:** Vigilant Privacy now tracks 1,505 data brokers across 9 categories—up from prior counts—indicating the broker universe is still growing, keeping demand for removal services robust.

## Market Summary
Privacy Bee's acquisition of EraseMe.app and its first-ever PCMag perfect score make it the standout mover this week, accelerating its consolidation of smaller competitors and widening its coverage lead. The full activation of California's DROP platform is the biggest structural shift in the market, creating a free government-backed alternative for CA residents that all paid services must now differentiate against.

# Competitor Insights — 2026-10-01

- Continues as the top-ranked personal data removal service since 2011, with 141M+ successful opt-out removals completed
- Pricing remains $129–$329/year depending on household size; re-checks every 90 days
- No major product announcements in the last 24–48 hours; service operating steadily
- California's DROP platform (launched January 2026, enforcement since August 2026) is a regulatory tailwind that may reduce perceived urgency of paid services for California residents

## Incogni (by Surfshark)
- Recently secured an Independent Limited Assurance Report from Deloitte confirming coverage of 420+ data brokers and 245M+ removal requests processed — strong trust signal
- Launched **Incogni Concierge**, a premium tier combining automated removals with specialist-run manual weekly searches; covers up to 5 adults per plan with dedicated phone/email support and phishing assistance
- Resubmits deletion requests every 60–90 days; plans start at $7.99/month
- Comparison articles (Incogni vs. BlackCloak) published in late September 2026 suggest Incogni is actively positioning itself against enterprise-adjacent privacy services

## Market / Regulatory Summary
- **Connecticut** data broker registration requirements took effect October 1, 2026, adding another state-level compliance layer for data brokers
- California's DROP platform (free universal opt-out for CA residents) has attracted hundreds of thousands of signups since August 2026 enforcement began — this represents a structural threat to paid services for California residents
- Over 20 US states now have comprehensive privacy laws with opt-out rights, compressing the addressable market for opt-out services in high-regulation states

## Market Summary
Privacy Bee's perfect PCMag score and Incogni's new Concierge tier are the standout competitive moves this week, with the industry broadly navigating the impact of California DROP and new state regulations that provide free government-backed alternatives. Services differentiating on breadth (Optery), premium human concierge (Incogni), and third-party validation (Privacy Bee, Incogni/Deloitte) appear best positioned.

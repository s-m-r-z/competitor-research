## Regulatory & Market
- **California AB 883 signed September 27, 2026**: Governor Newsom amended the Delete Act — registered data brokers must now process DROP deletion requests on a **30-day cycle** (down from 45 days); accelerates compliance burden on brokers and may increase removal velocity claims from paid services
- **Connecticut data broker law** active since October 1, 2026: brokers must register with CT Dept. of Consumer Protection; adds state-level compliance pressure and raises consumer awareness in a new market
- California DROP platform (launched Jan 2026, enforcement since Aug 1): free government tool for CA residents continues to pressure paid services in that state; AB 883 tightens its cycle, potentially making the free tool more effective

## Market Summary
Optery's back-to-back enterprise partnerships (GuidePoint Security on Sept 29, Carahsoft on Sept 23) mark the most significant competitive move of the past 48 hours, signaling a deliberate shift into B2B and government channels that most competitors haven't pursued. Simultaneously, California's AB 883 (signed Sept 27) tightening the DROP cycle to 30 days raises the bar for all services on removal speed claims, with Incogni's Deloitte-audited process and Optery's SOC 2 certification best positioned to meet enterprise and regulatory scrutiny.

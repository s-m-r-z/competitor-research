# Competitor Insights — 2026-09-24

## Optery (Surfshark Group)
- **NEW (Sep 23):** Optery and Carahsoft announced a strategic partnership making Carahsoft the Master Government Aggregator for Optery. This opens the entire U.S. Public Sector procurement channel — Optery for Business is now available through Carahsoft's reseller network and government procurement contracts (GSA, SEWP, etc.).
- Target use case: protecting elected officials, judges, prosecutors, law enforcement, and other high-risk personnel from data broker exposure — a segment with strong institutional budget and regulatory motivation.
- This is Optery's first major B2G (business-to-government) distribution move since the Surfshark acquisition (Sep 7, 2026). It signals an intentional enterprise/government expansion alongside the consumer product.
- Consumer Reports #1 ranking and Surfshark Group backing continue to serve as credibility anchors in comparison content.
- Pricing tiers unchanged: Basic (free), Core $3.25/month (annual, 250+ brokers), Extended ~$12.42/month (535+ brokers), Ultimate ~$20.70/month (630+ sites).
- **Watch:** Privacy Guides community has flagged that Optery now sends user data to OpenAI — this prompted a service delisting on the Privacy Guides forum. If this story gains broader pickup, it could dent Optery's credibility with privacy-conscious users and reviewers.

## Incogni (Surfshark Group)
- No new announcements in the past 24 hours.
- 55% off annual promotion remains active across coupon aggregators and partner placements as of September 2026.
- **Protect plan** (newest tier) bundles data removal with NordProtect identity theft protection (credit monitoring, dark web monitoring, identity remediation insurance). Currently U.S.-only.
- The Surfshark Group now holds Incogni + Optery + Ironwall — the only entity in the market with two separately ranked top-tier removal services (Incogni rated #1 overall value by security.org; Optery rated #1 effectiveness by Consumer Reports). This consolidation is the defining competitive dynamic of the category heading into Q4 2026.
- Coverage: 420+ brokers; Deloitte-audited removal process.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- California DROP compliance audit regulations proposed at August 7 CalPrivacy Board meeting remain in process. 345,000+ deletion requests submitted to date; $200/day per-consumer fines for broker non-compliance remain the enforcement lever.

- Carahsoft government distribution of Optery signals emerging B2G vertical as a competitive battleground; no other named competitor has a comparable public sector distribution agreement announced.

## Market Summary
The dominant story of the past 24 hours is Optery's Carahsoft partnership (announced September 23), which opens a government/public sector procurement channel and marks the first clear B2G vertical play in the data broker removal category. Combined with the Surfshark Group now controlling both Optery (#1 effectiveness) and Incogni (#1 value), the competitive landscape is consolidating rapidly around a single well-funded portfolio — creating significant positioning pressure on standalone services like DeleteMe, Privacy Bee, and Kanary heading into Q4 2026.

## Regulatory & Industry Context
- **California DROP "Delete Week" (Oct 12):** CalPrivacy promoted its first "Delete Week" highlighting the new free Delete Request and Opt-out Platform; over 560,000 Californians have registered. Broker-side compliance obligations took effect August 1, 2026.
- **Broker rejection data:** A Control D analysis found California data brokers rejected nearly 900,000 deletion requests before DROP launched; Live Data Technologies denied 45,853 of 45,865 requests — enforcement pressure is building.
- **CCPA 2027 expansion:** Starting January 1, 2027, the CCPA deletion right extends to data obtained from brokers/third parties, and online-only businesses must offer a web form or portal for privacy requests.
- **Connecticut & Delaware:** Connecticut's data broker oversight and geolocation sale ban took effect October 1, 2026. Delaware lowered its applicability threshold to 10,000 consumers.
- **FTC/Kochava settlement (May 2026):** Bars Kochava and its subsidiary from selling sensitive location data without affirmative consent — signals ongoing federal enforcement trend.
- **Radaris:** Domain now shows a court-ordered transfer notice; no working opt-out route currently available.

The biggest story this week is California's DROP system entering full enforcement mode with "Delete Week" driving consumer adoption (560,000 registrants), while data revealing widespread broker rejection of deletion requests signals growing regulatory scrutiny that could benefit paid removal services.

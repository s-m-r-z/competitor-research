# Competitor Insights — 2026-08-14

## Privacy Bee
- **Acquisition (Aug 12, 2026):** Privacy Bee acquired EraseMe.app, a personal data removal service with proprietary removal technology. EraseMe's tools will be integrated into Privacy Bee's platform to strengthen coverage for existing subscribers. This is the most significant M&A activity in the space this week.
- **PCMag Perfect Score:** Privacy Bee recently became the first-ever data removal service to earn a perfect 5-star rating from PCMag in the service's 44-year history (no competitor had previously exceeded 4.5 stars). This is a major credibility milestone and differentiator.
- **Coverage:** Automatically removes data from 1,117+ data brokers and people-search sites with continuous monitoring.

## Emerging / Regulatory Developments
- **California DROP Enforcement (Aug 1, 2026):** California's Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform entered its enforcement phase on August 1, 2026. Data brokers now face $200/day fines per deletion request if they fail to log into DROP every 45 days and process requests. This regulatory pressure is a market tailwind for paid removal services — it raises consumer awareness and validates the category.
- **Multi-State Expansion:** New Jersey launched an aggressive data broker regime; Connecticut expanded its framework to include centralized deletion; Vermont updated existing law. The regulatory landscape is tightening nationally.
- **Vigilant Privacy:** A newer entrant offering monitoring and removal from 2,359 brokers at $9.95/month — notably broad coverage at a low price point; worth tracking.

## Market Summary
The most notable activity this week is Privacy Bee's acquisition of EraseMe.app (Aug 12) and its unprecedented PCMag 5-star rating, signaling an aggressive move to consolidate technology and credibility simultaneously. The California DROP enforcement deadline (Aug 1) is driving heightened consumer interest across all players, with Incogni running deep discounts and Optery capitalizing on editorial momentum — competition for top-of-search positioning is at a seasonal high.

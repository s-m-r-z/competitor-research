# Competitor Insights — 2026-10-08

## Privacy Bee
- **PCMag perfect 5-star rating** (August 6, 2026): Privacy Bee became the first and only data removal service in PCMag history to receive a 5.0 "Exemplary" rating, along with Editors' Choice recognition. The review was conducted by Neil J. Rubenking, who first tested the service in 2021.
- Covers 1,128 data brokers with automated removals documented across more than 1,100 of them, the widest footprint in the industry per current reviews.
- PCMag competitive ranking as of the review: Privacy Bee 5.0, Optery 4.5, Incogni 4.5, Cloaked 4.0, Kanary 4.0, DeleteMe 3.5.
- Plans range from $96/year (Essentials) to $804/year (Signature); PCRisk independently tested the service and reported ~90% removal within weeks.

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- **Free Exposure Scanner now publicly available** with no account required: as of late September 2026, users can scan the searchable web for their exposed data in under a minute without signing up. Results feed into Incogni's paid "Custom Removals" workflow via the Exposures tab.
- Milestone: Incogni has now processed more than 245 million data removal requests since its 2022 launch.
- Covers 420+ data brokers and people-search sites; sends re-removal requests every 60 days for public brokers and every 90 days for private ones.
- Identified weakness (per Aura's June 2026 review): no dark web monitoring or breach alerts included.

## OneRep
- **Expanded to 550+ non-public data brokers** (press release May 13, 2026): in addition to its existing 300+ people-search site coverage, OneRep now processes removals from business-to-business data brokers that sell consumer data but don't display public profiles.
- Also added custom removal requests, allowing users to flag sites outside the standard coverage list.
- Reputational issue persists: the 2024 KrebsOnSecurity report about founder Dimitri Shelest's simultaneous operation of data broker sites continues to surface in 2026 review outlets, and Mozilla's severance (December 2025) is still cited as a trust signal.

## Optery (Surfshark)
- Holds a 4.5/5 PCMag rating and Editors' Choice from both CNET and PCMag (2022–2026).
- Ranked top paid performer in Consumer Reports' field test with 68% removal rate; EasyOptOuts (non-subscription) reached 65% at $19.99/year, the most cost-effective result in that test.
- Operating as an independent brand within the Surfshark group since the September 2, 2026 acquisition close.
- Pricing: $3.99/month (Core, 108 sites) to $24.99/month (Ultimate, 615+ sites).

## DeleteMe
- Rated "mid-level performer" in Consumer Reports testing; 3.5/5 on PCMag.
- No new product announcements found in the past 48 hours.
- Pricing remains stable: $129/year (1 person), $229/year (2 people), $329/year (4 people). Covers 750+ brokers on standard and 900+ monitored.
- Confirmed Black Friday/Cyber Monday sale period (November 21 – December 5, 2026) at 30% off — competitive pricing pressure period to monitor.

## Kanary
- Holds a 4.0/5 PCMag rating.
- No major product announcements found in the past 48 hours.
- Continues to differentiate on US people-search focus (Spokeo, WhitePages, BeenVerified) and automated re-submission; free tier and trial offer maintained.

## EasyOptOuts (Emerging Competitor)
- $19.99/year flat pricing; no subscription tiers.
- Reached 65% removal rate in Consumer Reports field test — the second-highest result behind Optery's top tier and the highest per-dollar outcome in the study.
- Gaining share in value-conscious segments; worth monitoring as a low-cost disruptor without VC backing.

## Removaly
- No longer an independent competitor; absorbed into DuckDuckGo's Privacy Pro service (acquisition completed 2023). ~50-site removal scope remains unchanged.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- **California DROP first enforcement cycle complete**: the first 45-day processing window under the Delete Request and Opt-out Platform closed around September 15, 2026. Data brokers operating in California are now legally obligated to process deletion requests from the state's free system; non-compliance carries penalties. This is the first month the enforcement regime is fully operational.
- Drop's free, single-request model covers 543+ registered California brokers and applies to CA residents only; Freedom of the Press Foundation recommends paid services (DeleteMe, Optery, EasyOptOuts) for non-California users.

## Market Summary
Privacy Bee's unprecedented PCMag 5-star rating is the sharpest competitive signal of the past two months, reshuffling the "best in class" narrative and putting pressure on Incogni and Optery (both at 4.5) to respond with product improvements or pricing moves; meanwhile California's DROP enforcement reaching full operational status marks the beginning of a new regulatory baseline that paid services will need to position against for California-resident customers.

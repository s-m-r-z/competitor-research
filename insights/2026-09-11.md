## Regulatory / Market Context
- **CalPrivacy DROP Enforcement:** The California Privacy Protection Agency issued a new enforcement advisory (FAQs) reinforcing that as of August 1, 2026, every registered California data broker must check the DROP platform every 45 days and process verified deletion requests within that window. Law firm Nelson Mullins published analysis calling it "another clear warning to data brokers." This ongoing compliance pressure benefits paid removal services whose value prop relies on broker non-compliance and re-listing.
- Sources: [Nelson Mullins advisory](https://www.nelsonmullins.com/insights/alerts/privacy_and_data_security_alert/all/calprivacy-drops-latest-drop-enforcement-advisory-faqs-and-another-clear-warning-to-data-brokers)

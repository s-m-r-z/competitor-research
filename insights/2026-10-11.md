# Competitor Insights — 2026-10-11

## DeleteMe
- No new product announcements or pricing changes in the past 24–48 hours.
- Continuing to market against California's DROP platform: emphasis on non-CA broker coverage, automated re-scanning, and proof-of-removal dashboards as differentiators from the free state tool.
- Block Party acquisition (March 2026) still operating as separate product; integration roadmap not yet announced.
- AI-powered Cyber Portal active and featured in review content; positions DeleteMe as a broader cybersecurity platform beyond broker removal.
- Stable pricing: $129/year (1 person), $229/year (2 people), $329/year (4 people). Coverage: 750+ brokers on standard plan.
- Independent testing (Consumer Reports benchmark, July 2026) rates DeleteMe as a mid-tier performer — a credibility headwind in 2026 buyer comparisons.

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- No new announcements in the past 24–48 hours.
- Now part of a three-product Surfshark privacy conglomerate alongside Optery (acquired Sept 7, 2026) and Ironwall (acquired 2024).
- Custom Removals feature (Unlimited plan): human-assisted removal for 2,000+ sites beyond the 420-broker automated list — key differentiator over most competitors.
- Pricing: Standard ~$95.88/year; Unlimited ~$179.88/year. Entry at $7.99/month billed annually.
- Deloitte ISAE 3000 assurance report and 76.6% third-party-measured removal rate (2025 peer-reviewed study) remain primary credibility markers.
- Integration with Surfshark's Optery acquisition could enable future cross-product bundles or feature sharing — watch for announcements.

## Optery (Surfshark)
- **Acquired by Surfshark on September 7, 2026** (majority stake; financial terms undisclosed). Optery continues operating independently with its own team, product, and technology.
- This is the most significant industry consolidation event of 2026: Surfshark now owns both Incogni and Optery, the two highest-rated automated removal services in independent benchmarks.
- International expansion signaled by Surfshark/TechRadar coverage — Optery currently US/Canada only.
- Coverage: 635+ brokers on standard plans; Custom Removals adds ~315 brokers (950+ total). Broadest verified coverage in the Consumer Reports July 2026 benchmark.
- Pricing unchanged post-acquisition: $3.99/month (Core) to $24.99/month (Ultimate).
- No signs yet of pricing changes or product mergers with Incogni; operating as a separate brand.

## Privacy Bee
- No new announcements in the past 24–48 hours.
- Three-tier pricing structure confirmed as of September 2026: Essentials (~$96/year), Pro (~$197/year), Signature (~$804/year).
- Essentials covers ~516 people-search sites; Pro expands to 1,131+ data brokers and 185,267 custom sites.
- No monthly payment option — all plans billed annually only.
- 30-day money-back guarantee; no free trial offered.
- PCMag 5-star Editors' Choice rating (earlier 2026) still prominent in comparison content; positions well in premium/family segments.

## Kanary
- No new announcements in the past 24–48 hours.
- **Compliance differentiators active (2026):** SOC 2, GDPR, and NIST CSF 2.0 certifications publicly listed — credentials Optery and Incogni do not highlight, giving Kanary an enterprise and security-buyer angle.
- Advanced plan includes address confidentiality enrollment — a proactive protection step beyond standard broker removal.
- Pricing: ~$14.99–$16.99/month on annual plan. 300+ brokers covered. Free tier and trial available.
- Lacks identity theft insurance (noted as a gap in TechRadar 2026 review).
- Consumer Reports benchmark (July 2026) rated Kanary mid-tier: ~16% profile removal rate within one week vs. Optery's 52%.

## OneRep
- No new announcements in the past 24–48 hours.
- Pricing (2026): Individual at $8.33/month billed annually; Individual Pro at $15.95/month; Family Pro at $29.95/month (up to 6 people).
- Coverage: 318 public people-search sites (standard); 877 total (Pro, adds 559 non-public brokers). 5-day free trial; 30-day money-back guarantee.
- Founder Dimitri Shelest's concurrent ownership stake in Nuwber (a data broker) continues to surface in third-party reviews — a sustained trust liability for privacy-aware buyers.

## Removaly

## Emerging / Market Developments
- **Surfshark privacy empire consolidation:** With Incogni, Optery, and Ironwall all under one parent, Surfshark is now the dominant infrastructure owner in automated data broker removal. Competitive pressure on standalone operators (DeleteMe, Privacy Bee, Kanary) will likely intensify if Surfshark bundles or cross-discounts these products.
- **California DROP enforcement in full swing:** As of August 1, 2026, all 600+ registered brokers must process deletion requests every 45 days. Early fines issued: LocateSmarter (~$116,490), Cybba ($52,400), SalesIntel Research ($36,400). ~450,000 Californians have submitted requests. Enforcement is being coordinated with Colorado and Connecticut.
- **EasyOptOuts ($19.99/year flat):** Consistently surfacing in 2026 buyer roundups as the standout budget option; Consumer Reports benchmark rated it among the top performers. Not a direct full-service competitor, but puts price pressure on the low end.
- **CrabClear:** Continues to claim 1,500+ broker coverage — roughly 3x Incogni's standard list. No independent verification found; monitoring for credibility evidence.

## Market Summary
No major product launches or pricing changes emerged in the past 24–48 hours. The dominant narrative remains Surfshark's September 7, 2026 acquisition of Optery, which has created a two-brand privacy conglomerate (Incogni + Optery) that now leads independent benchmarks — intensifying pressure on standalone competitors like DeleteMe, Privacy Bee, and Kanary to differentiate on trust, breadth, or compliance credentials.

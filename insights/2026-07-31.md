# Competitor Insights — 2026-07-31

## California DROP — Enforcement Day Zero

- **August 1, 2026 enforcement begins:** Today is the final day before all 600+ California-registered data brokers are legally required to process DROP deletion requests; as of midnight tonight the mandate is live
- 300,000+ California consumers have already submitted requests through the state's centralized deletion platform — the largest coordinated opt-out event in US history
- Non-compliance carries **$200/consumer/day** in fines with no statutory cap; a single missed 45-day retrieval cycle across the full enrollee pool represents theoretical liability exceeding $2B
- Enforcement track record is credible: CalPrivacy has already fined S&P Global ($62,600), Datamasters ($45,000), Accurate Append ($55,400), and National Public Data ($46,000) for registration violations — signaling they will act
- **Competitive impact:** DROP provides California residents a free state-run removal pathway limited to CA-registered brokers; paid services retain clear value for non-CA brokers, international coverage, and ongoing monitoring — expect marketing from all players leaning into this distinction starting tomorrow
- AI-derived inferences (e.g. "financially vulnerable," "likely pregnant") must be **permanently erased** under DROP — this is a higher bar than most paid services promise and will put pressure on the industry's data product definitions

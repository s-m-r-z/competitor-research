## Regulatory / Market Context
- California's DROP platform: as of August 1, 2026, all registered California data brokers must retrieve and process deletion requests from DROP at least every 45 days. Non-compliance triggers $200/day fines per unfulfilled request.
- 20+ US states now have comprehensive privacy laws granting consumers the right to opt out of data sales — broadening the total addressable market for opt-out services beyond California.
- Privacy Insight Solutions' opt-out directory (90+ high-priority brokers) was last audited September 11, 2026, correcting 30+ broker destinations — a signal of continued instability in broker opt-out mechanisms that strengthens the case for managed services.

# Competitor Insights — 2026-08-01

## California DROP — Enforcement Is Now Live

- **August 1, 2026: enforcement active as of today.** The California Privacy Protection Agency's DROP platform has crossed from "announced" to "operational" — all 600+ registered data brokers must now actively check the DROP system every 45 days and delete matching consumer data within 90 days
- The mandate applies to 300,000+ consumer requests already on file; failure to process carries **$200/consumer/day** in uncapped fines
- This is the single largest forced opt-out event in US history and transforms the competitive environment for paid removal services: DROP is free, covers CA-registered brokers, but does not cover non-CA brokers, international exposure, AI-derived inferences, people-search sites outside the registry, or provide ongoing monitoring and reporting
- Expect a wave of competitor marketing over the next 72 hours leaning on DROP's gaps to justify paid subscription value
- No statements or press releases spotted today from DeleteMe, Incogni, Optery, Privacy Bee, or OneRep specifically addressing DROP enforcement going live — marketing silence is notable given the opportunity

## Regulatory & Market Context
- **California DROP usage milestone**: Over 500,000 Californians registered as of August 25, 2026 (up from 300K reported in earlier tracking); accelerating adoption of the free state alternative continues to pressure lower-tier paid services
- **California enforcement action**: CPPA levied a $116,490 penalty against LocateSmarter for late registration and requiring partial SSNs before processing opt-outs — signals CPPA is actively enforcing compliance and may deter more broker friction tactics
- **Hidden opt-out pages**: A February 2026 Senate minority report identified Comscore, IQVIA Digital, Telesign, and 6sense as blocking their opt-out pages from Google; all have since removed the blocking code — industry compliance improving under scrutiny

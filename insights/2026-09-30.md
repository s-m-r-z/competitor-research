- US-only; leverages CCPA for automated removal requests — a coverage gap vs. Incogni (35+ countries)

## Regulatory & Market Context
- **Connecticut data broker law effective October 1, 2026 (tomorrow):** Data brokers must register with the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection starting October 1 — the first day of enforcement; this expands multi-state compliance pressure beyond California
- **New Jersey** advancing its own data broker registration and deletion framework — further multi-state momentum
- **California CalPrivacy enforcement actions in August 2026:**
  - *LocateSmarter* fined **$116,490** — first penalty under the Delete Act — for requiring SSN last-4 digits to process opt-outs (a CCPA violation)
  - *Cybba Inc.* fined **$52,400** for failing to register as a data broker by the January 31, 2025 deadline
- California DROP (Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform) fully operational since August 1, 2026; brokers must process requests every 45 days or face **$200/day per request** fines
- 2027 data broker registration fee in California set to rise to **$9,500** (up from ~$6,000 in 2026)

## Market Summary
The most significant near-term competitive event is Connecticut's data broker law taking effect October 1, 2026 — adding a new enforcement jurisdiction that should increase consumer awareness and drive service subscriptions across all major providers. Incogni retains the top-ranked position heading into Q4 2026, while Kanary's pivot to a mobile-first gamified model represents the most distinctive product strategy shift; no major pricing changes or product launches were detected from any competitor in the immediate 24–48 hour window.

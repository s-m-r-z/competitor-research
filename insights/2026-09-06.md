## Removaly
- **Status: Defunct.** Ceased independent operations in early 2022; technology acquired by DuckDuckGo in 2023, now powers DuckDuckGo Privacy Pro's Personal Information Removal feature. Not an active direct competitor.

## DuckDuckGo Privacy Pro (Emerging Bundled Competitor)
- Subscription ($9.99/month or $99.99/year) bundles VPN, identity theft restoration, and Personal Information Removal (powered by Removaly technology).
- Coverage: ~50+ data broker sites — materially weaker than dedicated services but the bundle value proposition is compelling for mainstream users.
- On-device removal model (data never leaves user's device) is a privacy-differentiating messaging angle.

## Regulatory & Market Context
- **California DROP Platform (August 1, 2026 — NOW IN EFFECT):** California's Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform requires all 500+ registered California data brokers to check DROP every 45 days and delete matched consumer records. Enforcement penalty: $200/day per unfulfilled request. Creates a free government alternative for California residents; may reduce paid-service urgency in CA while validating the market broadly.
- **FTC PADFAA Warning Letters (February 9, 2026):** FTC sent warning letters to 13 data brokers for potential PADFA violations involving data on military personnel. Up to $53,088 per violation. Signals continued federal regulatory pressure.
- **Senate JEC Report (February 27 + May 2026):** Found data broker breaches cost U.S. consumers $20+ billion. Report also exposed brokers hiding opt-out pages — multiple brokers improved opt-out processes after congressional pressure.
- **State Privacy Law Expansion:** 20 U.S. states now have comprehensive privacy laws in effect. New Jersey banned sale of sensitive data effective June 30, 2026. Connecticut's data broker registration takes effect October 1, 2026, with sale prohibition starting January 1, 2027.

## Market Summary
DeleteMe and Privacy Bee are the most aggressive movers in 2026, with DeleteMe expanding into social media privacy via acquisition and distribution, and Privacy Bee capturing the top PCMag rating while consolidating smaller competitors — together they are pulling away from the mid-tier field. The California DROP platform going live August 1 is the most significant regulatory event of the year and will be a key factor in how the industry positions its value proposition in Q3/Q4.

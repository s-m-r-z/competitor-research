# Competitor Insights — 2026-10-10

## DeleteMe
- No new product announcements in the past 24–48 hours.
- Block Party acquisition (announced March 24, 2026) continues to circulate in review content; both tools still operate as separate products with future integration signaled but not yet delivered.
- AI-powered Cyber Portal (announced earlier in 2026) is active — positions DeleteMe beyond standard broker removal into broader cybersecurity protection.
- IDScan breach investigation (September 2026): DeleteMe published a post on a dark web service "Nexus" claiming to hold scans of 150M+ people; DeleteMe used this to reinforce data-monitoring value prop.
- California DROP: DeleteMe published dedicated coverage of the state's free opt-out tool, framing paid services around non-CA coverage, ongoing re-scanning, and proof of removal — areas DROP doesn't cover.
- Stable pricing: $129/year (1 person), $229/year (2 people), $329/year (4 people). 750+ brokers on standard plan.

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- No breaking news in the past 24–48 hours.
- **Custom Removals feature** (launched in 2026): Unlimited, Family Unlimited, and Incogni Protect plan holders can now submit specific links for brokers outside Incogni's standard list — a notable differentiation from basic automated-only plans.
- Coverage tiers: Standard plan covers 420+ brokers (automated); Unlimited plan adds human-assisted removals across 2,000+ additional sites.
- Pricing: entry at $7.99/month billed annually. No free tier.
- Third-party validation: Deloitte ISAE 3000 assurance report (August 2025) remains the most-cited independent check; a 2025 peer-reviewed study reported a 76.6% removal rate, highest among services tested.
- Post-quantum WireGuard branding from Surfshark parent (launched Oct 7–8) continues to underpin broader privacy suite positioning, likely feeding Incogni marketing in near term.

## Optery (Surfshark)
- **Consumer Reports study response** (published ~July 2026, still circulating): Optery posted a formal statement disputing the study's methodology while noting it still ranked first — 52% of profiles removed within a week versus 16% for Kanary.
- Coverage: 635+ data brokers on standard plans; Custom Removals feature adds 315 additional brokers (950+ total). This is the broadest stated coverage among services tested in the Consumer Reports benchmark.
- Pricing: $3.99/month (Core, 108 sites) to $24.99/month (Ultimate, 615+ sites).
- Integration into Surfshark group (acquisition closed September 2, 2026) still operating as independent brand with no pricing or feature changes announced.

## Privacy Bee
- No new announcements found in the past 24–48 hours.
- 2026 roundup coverage positions it as "best for digital footprint removal." Starting price approximately $8.00/month; coverage spans 519–1,119+ brokers depending on plan tier.
- Manual + automated opt-out hybrid remains core differentiator. PCMag 5-star Editors' Choice rating (reported earlier in 2026) continues to appear in comparison content.
- Plans range from $96/year (Essentials) to $804/year (Signature).

## Kanary
- No product announcements found in the past 24–48 hours.
- **Compliance credentials** (active 2026): Kanary publicly lists SOC 2, GDPR, and NIST CSF 2.0 compliance — credentials Optery does not list, giving Kanary a differentiation angle for enterprise and security-conscious buyers.
- Advanced plan feature: includes address confidentiality enrollment (a proactive protection step, not just removal).
- Total opt-outs sent: 4.3M+ privacy requests over company history.
- Positioning: targets active/known threats and personalized site lists, versus Optery's broader automated sweep — different buyer use-cases.
- A Security.org comparison (published ~mid-September 2026) found Optery cheaper for routine cleanup; Kanary stronger for targeted threat response.

## OneRep

## Emerging / Market Developments
- **CrabClear** (emerging competitor): Positioning itself against DeleteMe and Incogni with claims of 1,500+ broker coverage — roughly 3x the scope of standard Incogni (420) and DeleteMe plans. Worth monitoring for pricing and credibility details; no independent third-party verification found.
- **California DROP platform** (live since January 1, 2026): Free government opt-out tool for CA residents; as of August 1, 2026, all registered brokers must process requests on a 45-day cycle. Covers 543+ registered brokers. Paid services are countering by emphasizing non-CA broker coverage, automated re-scanning, and proof-of-removal dashboards.
- **JEC Senate Report (February 2026):** Congressional examination of data broker opt-out obstacles; found most brokers improved opt-out access only after regulatory pressure. Continues to provide credibility backdrop for paid removal services.
- **Consumer Reports benchmark (July 2026):** Optery 52%+ removal rate leads; EasyOptOuts at $19.99/year flat is the standout value option; DeleteMe and Kanary rated mid-tier. This benchmark is now a primary buying-decision reference across comparison content.

## Market Summary
No major product launches or pricing changes emerged in the last 24–48 hours; the market is in a consolidation phase post-Surfshark/Optery integration (September 2026) and post-California DROP enforcement (August 2026). The key competitive dynamics are Incogni's Custom Removals differentiation versus Optery's breadth, DeleteMe's enterprise/AI pivot post-Block Party acquisition, and CrabClear's emerging coverage claims that — if independently validated — could pressure the mid-tier field.

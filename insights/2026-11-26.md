# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-26

## DeleteMe
- Ended enrollment for international/European plans as of September 2026; now US-only
- Current pricing: $129/year (1 person), $229 (2 people), $329 (household of 4)
- Florida local news (Nov 20, 2026) profiled a user citing ~85% reduction in spam after service — a provider claim, not independently verified
- Consumer Reports (2024 test, still widely cited) found only 27% listing removal rate — lowest among major services tested; no updated independent benchmark available

## Privacy Bee
- Earned PCMag's first-ever 5-star / Editors' Choice rating for a data removal service (August 6, 2026)
- Acquired EraseMe.app (August 12, 2026); former EraseMe users migrated onto Privacy Bee platform
- Now claims coverage of 1,128+ data brokers and 185,000+ custom sites
- Marketing aggressively off PCMag win; most positive coverage is company-sourced or in cooperation with Privacy Bee

## Kanary
- Rated 4.0/5 by PCMag as of August 2026 (per Privacy Bee press release comparison table)
- No significant new product or pricing announcements found for November 2026

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- Black Friday 2026 sale active: 58% off annual plans ahead of Nov 27 Black Friday
- Standard plan listed at $7.99/month billed annually ($95.88/year)
- 420+ brokers listed; 366 of those are US-only per independent review (November 22, 2026)
- Parent company Surfshark now owns both Incogni and Optery (see below)

## Optery
- Majority stake acquired by Surfshark (announced September 7, 2026); operating independently with separate teams, products, and technology
- Surfshark's data removal portfolio now includes Incogni, Optery, and Ironwall
- Free tier available with exposure reports and self-service opt-out instructions
- Paid plans: Core from $39/year, Ultimate at $249/year
- Rechecks opt-outs every 30 days per company claims

## Emerging Services
- **Sirveil**: Appeared on Product Hunt with automated removal requests tied to state privacy laws; date unconfirmed — monitoring warranted
- **Cloaked**: Expanded from data removal into a broader privacy platform adding "Privacy Scores" in July 2026
- **BlackCloak**: Redesigned its Data Broker Removal (DBR) feature in May 2026; now reports removals per site rather than per profile

## Regulatory Landscape
- **California DROP**: State one-click deletion portal now covers 665+ registered data brokers; since August 1, 2026, brokers must check and act on requests every 45 days
- **CA fee increase**: Annual data broker registration fee raised from $6,000 to $9,500 effective 2027 (58% increase)
- **Upcoming rules**: CA Privacy Protection Agency advancing rulemaking for third-party DROP compliance audits and may formally name Global Privacy Control (GPC) in CCPA regulations

## Market Summary
The most significant structural development of the past period is Surfshark's consolidation play — owning both Incogni and Optery creates a dominant multi-tier offering spanning VPN, automated removal, and enterprise privacy tools. With Black Friday promotions now live (Incogni at 58% off) and Privacy Bee capitalizing on its PCMag 5-star win, the market is in an aggressive acquisition cycle heading into Q4 — services without a clear differentiation story or distribution channel face increasing pressure.

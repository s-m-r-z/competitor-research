# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-13

## DeleteMe (Abine)
- **ISO 27001:2022 certification** achieved (confirmed October 9, 2026) — first major third-party security audit milestone for the company; being used in B2B pitches
- **Risk Scan** feature (launched September 14, 2026) now available to business admins: scans for exposed PII of unboarded employees before purchasing coverage; free to use pre-sale
- Privacy masking tools (email, phone, credit card masking) currently **paused** while a platform update is finalized — may cause friction for new and existing users
- Continues to position California DROP as complementary ("works better with DeleteMe") rather than a threat; DROP explainer still active on their blog

- **Cloaked** (well-funded at $375M) expanding beyond data broker removal into broader identity protection; not yet a direct competitor but worth watching

## Regulatory & Market Context
- **California DROP platform** went fully operational for data brokers on **August 1, 2026** (required to access the platform at least every 45 days). Over 450,000 Californians have submitted deletion requests; 600+ brokers are registered.
- **CPPA advanced formal rulemaking** (August 6–7, 2026) on opt-out preference signals (including Global Privacy Control) and new audit requirements for data brokers — increased enforcement posture expected.
- New Jersey, Connecticut, and Vermont have each expanded or introduced state-level data broker frameworks in 2026, broadening the regulatory tailwind for removal services.

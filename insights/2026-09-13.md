## Regulatory / Market Context

- **CalPrivacy enforcement blitz intensifying in September:** The California Privacy Protection Agency issued a formal enforcement decision against SalesIntel Research, Inc. (a Virginia-based data broker), requiring a $36,400 fine and practice changes for failure to timely register with the Data Broker Registry. This is part of an ongoing DROP enforcement strike force launched in late 2025.
- **DROP compliance fees increasing:** Data broker registration and access fees rise to $9,500 for the 2027 registration period — up $3,500 from 2026 — increasing friction for smaller data brokers and potentially consolidating the brokerage industry.
- **345,000+ deletion requests submitted** to DROP since its August 1, 2026 launch. Despite the volume, compliance rate among registered brokers remains low (~9%), a gap all paid removal services are actively using in marketing.
- The broader data broker industry is estimated at $300B+ globally. Nineteen US states now have comprehensive privacy laws, with 16 already in effect.
- Sources: [CalPrivacy enforcement action Sept 2026](https://privacy.ca.gov/2026/09/calprivacy-continues-enforcement-blitz-with-action-against-virginia-data-broker/), [Clark Hill DROP analysis](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/is-your-business-a-data-broker-californias-drop-goes-live-and-calprivacy-continues-to-enforce-delete-act/), [Nelson Mullins DROP advisory](https://www.nelsonmullins.com/insights/alerts/privacy_and_data_security_alert/all/calprivacy-drops-latest-drop-enforcement-advisory-faqs-and-another-clear-warning-to-data-brokers/)

The competitive landscape enters the week of GSX 2026 with Surfshark Group's Incogni running a 55%-off promotional push while Optery's acquisition integration quietly continues — collectively consolidating the most diverse removal portfolio in the market.

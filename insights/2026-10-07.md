# Competitor Insights — 2026-10-07

## Optery
- **Acquired by Surfshark** (transaction closed September 2, 2026; announced September 7, 2026): Surfshark Holdings acquired a majority stake in Optery, the California-based data removal service known for its detailed screenshot reporting and broad US broker coverage.
- Optery will continue to operate independently within the Surfshark group — separate teams, products, and technology — alongside Incogni and Ironwall.
- Optery retains its CNET and PCMag "Editors' Choice" awards for personal data removal (2022–2026), the only service to hold both.

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- **New pricing plan added September 5, 2026**: Incogni expanded its lineup from 4 to 5 plans; the new tier's details were not fully disclosed but pricing now spans $7.99–$22.99/month.
- Current plans: Standard ($7.99/mo billed annually), Unlimited ($14.99/mo), Family Standard ($15.99/mo avg), Family Unlimited ($22.99/mo avg).
- Covers 420+ data brokers; now the anchor product in Surfshark's three-brand data-removal portfolio (Incogni + Optery + Ironwall).

## DeleteMe
- Pricing is stable: $129/year (1 person), $229/year (2 people), $329/year (4 people).
- A **Black Friday/Cyber Monday sale** (30% off annual and two-year plans) is confirmed for November 21–December 5, 2026 — worth monitoring as a competitive pricing signal.
- Standard plan covers ~100 US brokers; premium tiers cover 750+.

## OneRep
- Continues to face reputational fallout from the 2024 KrebsOnSecurity report revealing the founder simultaneously operated data broker sites.
- Mozilla formally severed ties with OneRep on December 17, 2025.
- Multiple 2026 review outlets actively disqualify OneRep from best-of rankings on trust grounds.

## Kanary
- Positioned as a strong pick for US users in 2026 roundups; covers 300+ brokers with continuous re-appearance monitoring.
- Differentiated focus on people-search engines (Spokeo, WhitePages, BeenVerified) and automated re-submission workflows.

## Privacy Bee
- Maintains 1,000+ data broker coverage, the widest footprint in the industry per current reviews.
- No major product announcements found in the last 48 hours.

## Removaly
- No longer an independent competitor; DuckDuckGo absorbed Removaly into its Privacy Pro service (acquisition completed 2023). ~50-site removal scope.

## Market / Regulatory Context
- **California DROPA (Delete Request and Opt-out Platform)** launched in early 2026 allowing CA residents to submit deletion requests to 543+ registered data brokers via a single state-run tool — a potential pressure point on paid services marketing to California consumers.
- A **U.S. Senate JEC report on data brokers** was released February 27, 2026, adding legislative momentum to the space.
- Data brokers are being accused of **hiding opt-out pages from Google search results**, which could increase consumer frustration and demand for automated removal services.

## Market Summary
The biggest structural shift of recent weeks is Surfshark's consolidation of three data-removal brands (Incogni, Optery, Ironwall) under one ownership umbrella, effectively creating the largest privacy-services conglomerate in the space; simultaneously, California's state-run opt-out platform and mounting congressional scrutiny are raising the floor for consumer expectations and adding regulatory tailwind across the industry.

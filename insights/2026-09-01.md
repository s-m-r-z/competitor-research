## Regulatory Environment — CalPrivacy Dual Enforcement (Aug 11–13, 2026)
- **LocateSmarter fined $116,490** (Aug 11) — CalPrivacy's first enforcement action combining both CCPA and Delete Act violations; the opt-out form required consumers to submit their SSN last four digits, which CalPrivacy ruled as excessive data collection for an opt-out request
- **Cybba, Inc. fined $52,400** (Aug 13) — Boston-based ad-tech data broker fined for failing to register with California's Data Broker Registry; ordered to connect to DROP and publish privacy-request metrics
- Two enforcement actions in three days signals CalPrivacy is moving from rulemaking into active enforcement; data brokers that make opt-outs burdensome are now primary targets
- **Strategic implication:** Services like DeleteMe, Optery, and Incogni that emphasize automated, frictionless opt-out workflows benefit from every enforcement action that penalizes manual/complex broker opt-out flows
- Sources: [CalPrivacy LocateSmarter announcement](https://privacy.ca.gov/2026/08/calprivacy-brings-first-action-against-a-data-broker-under-both-the-ccpa-and-delete-act/), [CalPrivacy Cybba announcement](https://privacy.ca.gov/2026/08/calprivacy-announces-second-data-broker-enforcement-action-in-less-than-a-week/), [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/handful-opt-outs-and-six-figure-fine-calprivacys-message-data-brokers)

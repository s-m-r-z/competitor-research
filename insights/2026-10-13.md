## Market / Regulatory Developments
- **Connecticut Public Act 26-64 now in effect (October 1, 2026):** Connecticut's major new data broker law (SB 4) became enforceable this month. Key provisions: geolocation data sales ban, mandatory data broker registration framework, surveillance pricing restrictions, genetic data protections, and facial recognition/ALPR safeguards. Data broker registration deadline is January 1, 2027. This is the most significant new state-level regulatory event this week and will affect all services operating in Connecticut.
- **Maryland HB1089:** Legislation to establish a data broker registry with mandatory Attorney General registration; effective date listed as October 1, 2026, but passage unconfirmed — monitor for final status.
- **Senate JEC report (February 2026):** A Joint Economic Committee minority report (Ranking Member Hassan) argues data broker opt-out mechanisms are "largely invisible and incomplete" per the FTC's own characterization. Findem (one covered broker) subsequently improved its opt-out process after engagement with the Committee.
- **Connecticut CTDPA threshold change (July 1, 2026):** CTDPA now applies to businesses processing data of 35,000+ CT consumers (down from 100,000), bringing significantly more companies under state privacy law.
- **Pricing overview for context:** Individual data removal services in 2026 range from ~$3–$25/month; Optery offers a free basic tier. Competitive pressure on mid-tier pricing continues.

# Competitor Insights — 2026-10-18

## Privacy Bee
- First data removal service to earn a perfect 5/5 PCMag rating (Editors' Choice, August 5, 2026); competitors Optery and Incogni rated 4.5 in the same roundup.
- Acquired EraseMe.app in mid-August 2026, expanding removal automation and monitoring; existing Privacy Bee subscribers unaffected, EraseMe.app customers being migrated.
- Platform now covers 1,128 data brokers with automated removals across 1,100+ of them and 185,000+ custom sites (per PCMag review).
- No new October 2026 announcements found; August activity remains the dominant news cycle.

## Optery
- Ranked No. 177 on the 2026 Inc. 5000 list (announced August 11, 2026) — No. 2 in the Security category — with 1,906% three-year revenue growth.
- CNET named Optery best overall data removal service of 2026 (last modified July 7, 2026).
- Expanded to Australia, New Zealand, and South Africa in March 2026, following its Canada expansion in late 2025.
- Reported Surfshark/Incogni acquisition (September 2026) still unconfirmed; no official announcement found as of today.
- PCWorld review (August 2026) notes strong free scan but says full cleanup value requires a paid plan upgrade.

## DeleteMe
- No significant news in the past 24–48 hours.
- 2026 pricing confirmed: $129/year (1 person), $229/year (2 people), $329/year (4 people); no monthly billing option available.
- Third-party review notes data typically reappears within 3–6 months post-cancellation as brokers re-acquire from public records.
- Some brokers still require optional government ID upload for removal completion — a friction point cited in 2026 reviews.

## Incogni
- No new October 2026 product announcements or pricing changes found.
- Last significant marketing push was holiday/Black Friday deals (55–58% off annual plans); reported $6.49–$7.00/month pricing on annual plan.
- A possible Surfshark acquisition of Optery (reported September 2026) would combine Incogni and Optery under one ownership — still unconfirmed; monitor closely.
- Wikipedia page and Tom's Hardware Italia review describe the two-section removal progress dashboard ("Data broker" / "Requests") as its main UX differentiator.

## OneRep
- May 13, 2026: Expanded coverage to 550+ non-public data brokers in addition to existing 300+ people-search sites; available via both the OneRep API and individual subscriptions.
- Founder Dimitri Shelest framed the expansion as a response to growing consumer awareness of data monetization practices.
- Ongoing reputational risk from 2024 Krebs on Security investigation (ties to Belarus/Cyprus and people-search site history) continues to appear in competitor reviews.
- No new news since May 2026.

## Kanary
- SOC 2 Type 1 attestation completed March 2026 — a trust-building milestone being cited in 2026 reviews.
- Three pricing tiers: free Community plan (self-guided), $9.99/month Professional (automated), $50/month Advanced (full management, also serves UK).
- Reviewers consistently flag slow removal speeds: 30–90 days for initial results, only 6 profiles removed in first two weeks in one test.
- Claims to monitor 68,000+ unique sites, but methodology for that figure is not disclosed; coverage of 300–350 broker/people-search sites confirmed.
- No standalone company news found; appears primarily in comparison roundups.

## Removaly

## Regulatory & Industry Context
- **California DROP enforcement:** Mandatory broker compliance (retrieval + deletion every 45 days) took effect August 1, 2026. A Control D report found California brokers rejected ~890,000 deletion requests before DROP launched, with 22 brokers rejecting 90%+ of requests — regulatory pressure building.
- **DROP enrollment:** More than 300,000 Californians enrolled as of June 2026; October "Delete Week" (week of Oct 12) drove additional sign-ups to ~560,000 (per yesterday's data).
- **Connecticut:** Data broker oversight law and geolocation sale ban took effect October 1, 2026.
- **CCPA 2027:** Starting January 1, 2027, CCPA deletion rights extend to data obtained from third-party brokers; online businesses must offer a dedicated privacy request portal.

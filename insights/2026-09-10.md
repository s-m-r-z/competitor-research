# Competitor Insights — 2026-09-10

## Optery ⚡ MAJOR NEWS
- **Surfshark Acquisition (September 7, 2026):** Surfshark Group announced the acquisition of a majority stake in Optery, the US-based data removal service. Financial terms were not disclosed. This is the most significant market development in the last 48 hours.
- Surfshark now controls both **Incogni** and **Optery** under one corporate umbrella (alongside Ironwall), creating a dominant two-brand data removal portfolio with combined coverage across hundreds of brokers.
- Optery will continue operating independently with its own team, product, and technology. The companies plan to share expertise on data-broker coverage, search-related removals, and new privacy tools.
- Strategic rationale: Surfshark cited the AI-driven world as driving demand for stronger data control, particularly as personal information increasingly surfaces in AI-generated search summaries.
- Sources: [Surfshark official blog](https://surfshark.com/blog/surfshark-acquires-optery), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-services/surfshark-bolsters-its-privacy-empire-with-optery-data-removal-acquisition), [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/surfshark-just-bought-the-tool-that-could-erase-your-name-from-hundreds-of-data-brokers-lists-2000808180)

## Market Summary
The defining story this week is Surfshark Group's acquisition of Optery (announced September 7), which gives a single corporate parent control over both Incogni and Optery — two of the most-reviewed data removal services in the market. This consolidation mirrors Privacy Bee's absorption of EraseMe.app last month and signals an accelerating M&A phase in the sector. DeleteMe is simultaneously pushing hard into enterprise/B2B positioning ahead of its GSX 2026 appearance, differentiating on AI-threat intelligence and executive protection rather than competing purely on broker count.

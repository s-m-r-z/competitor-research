## Industry / Regulatory
- California AB 883 signed September 27, 2026: tightens DROP processing window from 45 days to **30 days** — a meaningful enforcement tightening that makes all removal services more accountable in CA.
- DROP (Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform) now has 300,000+ enrolled Californians; required for registered brokers since August 1, 2026.
- **Radaris reportedly offline**: a directory entry from late October 2026 states the domain was transferred by court order — potentially reducing active broker count for all services.
- A 2025 academic study (cited in Gizmodo's October 2026 coverage) found automated removal services "relatively ineffective" as a category, adding pressure on all players to demonstrate real-world results.
- Cybersecurity Awareness Week (Oct 24 week) drove mainstream press coverage of data removal, with 9to5Mac noting Apple's built-in tools are insufficient alone — positive for the paid service category.

## Market Summary
The dominant story this week is California's regulatory tightening (AB 883 cutting broker response time to 30 days) combined with Radaris going offline via court order, both of which validate the opt-out space while raising the bar for demonstrable results. Optery and Incogni are the most actively iterating products right now, while Kanary's pivot to a mobile-app model (Copilot) is the biggest strategic move among mid-tier players.

# Competitor Insights — 2026-08-20

## Privacy Bee
- **MAJOR**: Privacy Bee became the first and only data removal service to earn a perfect 5-star (Exemplary) rating and Editors' Choice from PCMag (published August 6, 2026) — a historic first in PCMag's 44 years of testing, where no prior service had exceeded 4.5 stars.
- **ACQUISITION**: Privacy Bee acquired EraseMe.app, adding new technology to its platform shortly after the PCMag milestone.
- Now covers **1,128 data brokers** and supports removals from **185,000+ custom sites** — the most extensive coverage cited in any current review.
- PCMag ratings as of August 5, 2026: Privacy Bee 5.0 · Optery 4.5 · Incogni 4.5 · Cloaked 4.0 · Kanary 4.0 · IDX Complete 4.0 · PrivacyHawk 4.0 · DeleteMe 3.5 · Aura 3.5.

## Incogni

- Review refreshed August 12, 2026, citing "best overall value" for automated recurring removal requests.
- Coverage remains 420+ data brokers; Deloitte third-party assurance report (published August 2025) confirms 245M+ removal requests processed since January 2022.

## Optery
- PCMag rating: **4.5 stars**; user rating 4.8 stars.
- Automates removals from 600+ sites with real before-and-after screenshots as proof of deletion.
- Offers a free exposure report as an entry point; multiple customer support channels cited as a differentiator.
- Optery has published a comparative "OneRep Review" piece on its own blog, signaling active head-to-head marketing against OneRep.

## OneRep
- Ongoing reputational risk: multiple 2026 reviews continue to flag the CEO's past affiliation with data broker businesses as a disqualifying concern.
- Covers 318 data brokers; allows unlimited aliases, addresses, and phone numbers per account.
- Being actively targeted in comparative content by Optery and Incogni.

## DeleteMe
- PCMag rating: **3.5 stars** — notably below the market leaders now.
- Pricing: $129/year per person; manual, risk-prioritized approach; removes data from ~122 sites (one of the lower site counts in current benchmarks).
- Still cited as one of the most established services with 90-day recheck cycles.

## Kanary
- PCMag rating: **4.0 stars**.
- **PRODUCT PIVOT**: Kanary has transitioned from a web-based service to a mobile app called **Kanary Copilot** — new users can only access the Copilot app (iOS only; Android waitlist open). Web version remains for existing subscribers.
- Copilot reframes privacy as bite-sized weekly tasks (~10 min/week), targeting habit formation rather than pure automation.
- Pricing: $14.99/month (annual) or $16.99/month; covers 300+ data brokers; achieves 95–100% removal by day 90.

## Removaly
- **SHUT DOWN**: Removaly is no longer an active service as of 2026. Multiple competitor blogs now publish "Removaly alternatives" roundups capitalizing on its closure.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- **California DROP goes live (August 1, 2026)**: The Delete Request and Opt-out Platform now requires all 600+ registered data brokers to process deletion requests every 45 days. Over 450,000 Californians have already submitted requests — creating immediate tailwinds for opt-out services marketing to California users.
- **CPPA rulemaking advancing** (August 6–7, 2026 board meeting): California Privacy Protection Agency voted to advance formal rulemaking on opt-out preference signals (Global Privacy Control / GPC) and new audit requirements for data brokers; also increased broker registration fees.
- **Multi-state expansion**: New Jersey, Connecticut, and Vermont all advanced data broker legislation in 2026; 12 states now mandate GPC as a binding universal opt-out signal.

## Market Summary
This week's dominant story is Privacy Bee's historic PCMag perfect-score milestone and its EraseMe.app acquisition — a strong brand moment that raises competitive pressure on Optery and Incogni, which hold the second-tier 4.5-star positions. The California DROP platform going live August 1 and CPPA's advancing enforcement posture represent structural tailwinds for the entire industry, likely to drive increased consumer awareness and service adoption in Q3 2026.

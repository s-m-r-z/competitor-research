# Competitor Insights — 2026-09-08

## BREAKING: Surfshark Acquires Optery (September 7, 2026)
- **Acquisition Announced:** Surfshark Group confirmed the acquisition of US-based data removal service Optery on September 7, 2026. No financial terms were disclosed.
- **Strategic Implication:** Surfshark Group now owns **both Incogni and Optery** — two of the top-rated data removal services — alongside Ironwall. This is the most significant consolidation event in the data broker opt-out industry to date.
- **Independence Maintained:** Optery will continue operating with its own team, product, and technology — mirroring the approach Surfshark took with Incogni.
- **Competitive Threat:** Surfshark Group now controls dual premium removal brands targeting different customer segments: Incogni (automated, set-and-forget, lower cost) and Optery (visual proof of every removal, premium pricing, transparency-focused). This dual-brand strategy can capture across a wider willingness-to-pay spectrum.
- **Coverage Combined:** Incogni covers 420–2,420+ brokers; Optery covers hundreds of sites with full visual verification. Together they represent near-comprehensive market coverage under one parent.
- **Watch for:** Potential integration of Optery's scanning technology into Incogni, cross-sell opportunities, bundled offerings via Surfshark One+, and possible price competition if brands begin overlapping.

## Regulatory & Market Context
- **California DROP (Active since August 1, 2026):** All 500+ registered California data brokers must now check the DROP platform at least every 45 days and process deletion requests within 45 days. Non-compliance triggers $200/day/request fines.
- **Connecticut Data Broker Registration:** Takes effect October 1, 2026 — the next near-term state-level regulatory milestone for the industry.
- **Industry Scale:** The global data broker market exceeds $300 billion; 442+ data brokers are listed for opt-out management as of 2026 — underscoring the ongoing need for automated removal services despite expanding government alternatives.

## Market Summary
The Surfshark Group acquisition of Optery (announced September 7, 2026) is the most significant consolidation event in the data broker opt-out industry to date, giving one parent company ownership of both Incogni and Optery and creating a dominant two-brand platform covering the automated and verified-removal segments simultaneously — Privacy Bee and DeleteMe remain the most credible independent alternatives, but the competitive landscape has fundamentally shifted overnight.

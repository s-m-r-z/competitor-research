## Emerging / Regulatory Developments

### California DROP Platform — Key Update
- California's DELETE Request and Opt-Out Platform (DROP) is now actively processing requests; ~590,000 Californians enrolled, covering 680+ registered brokers.
- Average user is seeing ~200 broker deletions via the free platform.
- Governor Newsom signed **AB 883**, shortening broker DROP processing from every 45 days to every 30 days.
- CalPrivacy Board is holding a November meeting to weigh OOPS (privacy notices and friction reduction) and plans formal rulemaking on data broker audits with a comment period closing in December.

### Connecticut Data Broker Law
- Connecticut's amended data broker rules took effect October 1, 2026; mandatory broker registration begins January 1, 2027.
- Penalties can reach $200/day per consumer per violation; audit requirements start in 2031.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- **California DROP milestone (Nov 5, 2026):** The California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA) confirmed DROP is operating as planned after its first 90-day compliance milestone. On average, users have seen ~200 brokers comply with deletion requests. Only ~30% of registered brokers were processing requests in week one; enforcement penalties of $200/request/day are in effect since August 2026.
- Approximately 450,000 consumer deletion requests have been submitted through DROP to date.
- Next major milestone: independent third-party compliance audits begin January 1, 2028.
- Some data brokers were found to be obscuring opt-out pages from Google search results; four named companies have since removed the code, though the broader practice persists industry-wide.
- BlackCloak redesigned its Data Broker Removal feature in May 2026 — a niche B2B entrant gaining traction in the enterprise/executive protection space.

## Market Summary
The Surfshark/Incogni acquisition of Optery is consolidating the U.S. data removal market, with Surfshark now owning two of the most-cited services; Incogni's aggressive Black Friday pricing (58% off) signals heightened price competition heading into Q4. California's DROP platform is adding regulatory pressure on all brokers and reshaping the value proposition of paid removal services, as the free state tool now handles ~200 brokers per request — forcing vendors to differentiate on breadth, speed, and non-California coverage.

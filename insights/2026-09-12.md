## Surfshark Group (Incogni + Optery)

- **Acquisition integration narrative taking shape:** Five days after Surfshark's September 7 announcement of its Optery acquisition, the company has articulated a clear dual-brand strategy — Optery will remain a standalone product and team, not folded into Incogni. Surfshark will cross-leverage the two brands across its combined VPN + privacy subscriber base (estimated tens of millions globally).
- Surfshark now controls three data-removal properties: **Incogni** (automated, 420+ brokers), **Optery** (300+ brokers, screenshot-verified removals), and **Ironwall** (executive/public-sector focus, acquired 2024). This positions the group as the most diversified privacy conglomerate in the removal space.
- **Incogni's new "Protect" plan** bundles data removal with NordProtect identity theft protection (credit monitoring, dark web monitoring, identity remediation insurance). Currently US-only. Signals an upsell strategy targeting identity-protection spend on top of the core removal service.
- Incogni pricing: Standard Annual at ~$7.99/month ($95.88/yr), Unlimited Annual at ~$14.99/month. Deloitte Independent Limited Assurance certification remains a key trust differentiator.

## Regulatory / Market Context

- **California DROP fully live:** As of August 1, 2026, all 654+ registered California data brokers must check the DROP platform every 45 days and process deletion requests within that window. Over 345,000 deletion requests submitted to date. Only 9% of brokers are fully compliant by some measures — this compliance gap is a sustained talking point for paid removal services, which position themselves as filling the enforcement vacuum.
- The California Privacy Protection Agency Board is meeting to discuss proposed DROP compliance audit regulations and additional amendments — further enforcement likely, which could improve free opt-out effectiveness and pressure paid service value propositions long-term.
- Sources: [CalMatters DROP article](https://calmatters.org/economy/technology/2026/01/californians-block-personal-data/), [Alston & Bird DROP analysis](https://www.alstonprivacy.com/drop-is-coming-due-what-californias-delete-act-means-for-data-brokers-in-august/), [Taft Privacy DROP update](https://www.privacyanddatasecurityinsight.com/2026/08/just-dropped-a-data-broker-law-update/)

# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-20

## DeleteMe
- Acquired Block Party (social media privacy tool) in March 2026 to combine data broker removal with social media exposure protection across Facebook, Google, Venmo, Instagram, LinkedIn, and X.
- Financial terms were not disclosed; both products continue to operate separately while integration is planned.
- DeleteMe reports 35M+ records removed to date and has covered California's DROP platform in its own blog.
- No new product announcements or pricing changes found in the past 24–48 hours.

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- Now covers 420+ data brokers; pricing holds at ~$7.99/month ($95.88/year) for the Standard plan.
- Recent reviews (TechRadar, inkl, 2026) rate it a solid choice; critics note it sends opt-out requests to all brokers in its network regardless of whether they hold the user's data.
- No new feature launches or pricing changes detected in the past 24–48 hours.

## OneRep
- On May 13, 2026 expanded coverage to 550+ non-public data brokers, available via the OneRep API and Pro plans — the most significant product update this company has had in 2026.
- Pricing as of October 2026: $99.96/year (Individual) to $191.40/year (Individual Pro).
- No new announcements detected in the past 24–48 hours.

## Optery
- Introduced map image blurring for Google and Apple Maps (March 2026).
- Extended tier pairs automated tools with human privacy agents ("humans + machines" model); Maximize Removals and Expanded Reach features send additional removal requests beyond standard coverage.
- Pricing tiers: Free Basic, $39, $149, and $249/year as of mid-2026.
- No new announcements detected in the past 24–48 hours.

## Privacy Bee
- Pro plan covers 1,000+ data brokers and people search sites, though third-party reviews give conflicting coverage numbers (300–1,000+).
- Features include a proprietary quick-delete playbook, breach monitoring, search engine result monitoring, and street-view blurring.
- No new product announcements detected in the past 24–48 hours.

## Kanary
- Free tier offers automated scans and removals on a limited site set; paid tier adds monthly progress reports and monitoring via Google, Twitch, and Discord account integrations.
- Pricing listed at ~$179.88/year in Consumer Reports' 2024 evaluation; more recent sources show ~$29/month.
- No new announcements detected in the past 24–48 hours.

## Removaly
- No new activity or announcements found in the past 24–48 hours.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- California's DELETE Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP) — launched January 1, 2026, broker compliance required from August 1, 2026 — has reached 300,000+ California residents signed up (as of June 2026) and covers 581 registered brokers. This free state alternative continues to pressure paid services on value proposition for California residents.
- Industry pricing snapshot (2026): DeleteMe ~$129/year (1 person), Incogni ~$95.88/year, OneRep ~$99.96/year, Optery $39–$249/year, Kanary ~$179.88/year.

## Market Summary
No significant competitor product launches or pricing changes were detected in the past 24–48 hours; the most recent material developments remain DeleteMe's Block Party acquisition (March 2026), OneRep's expansion to 550+ non-public brokers (May 2026), and the ongoing impact of California's free DROP platform, which continues to reshape the value case for paid data-removal subscriptions among California consumers.

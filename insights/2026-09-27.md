# Competitor Insights — 2026-09-27

## Optery
- **MAJOR: Acquired by Surfshark** — Surfshark completed its acquisition of Optery, which specializes in finding and removing personal data from 1,360+ US data broker and people-search sites. Optery joins Surfshark as an independent service, marking Surfshark's second privacy-focused acquisition (following its 2024 purchase of Ironwall, aimed at government employees and executives). Surfshark frames the acquisition as a response to growing demand for data control in an AI-driven world where personal information increasingly surfaces in AI-generated summaries.
- Running a **Fall 2026 Sale** — promo code `FALL2026` offers 20% off at checkout.
- Pricing tiers remain: Free ($0), Core ($3.99/mo / $39/yr), Extended ($14.99/mo / $149/yr), Ultimate ($24.99/mo / $249/yr).
- Holds PCMag Editors' Choice; Consumer Reports study showed Optery outperformed all competitors including DeleteMe by 41 percentage points in removal effectiveness.

## Market Summary
The headline event this week is Surfshark's acquisition of Optery, consolidating another top-ranked removal service into a major VPN/privacy suite — a pattern that mirrors NordVPN's ownership of Incogni and signals accelerating vertical integration in the consumer privacy space. Simultaneously, California's DROP platform surpassing 500K users and rising regulatory enforcement are beginning to commoditize DIY opt-out workflows, which may pressure mid-tier paid services to differentiate on depth, speed, and high-risk use cases rather than breadth alone.

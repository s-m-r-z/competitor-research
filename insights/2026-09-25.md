# Competitor Insights — 2026-09-25

## Optery
- **Acquired by Surfshark** (announced September 7, 2026): Surfshark Group acquired a majority stake in Optery, adding it to a privacy portfolio that already includes Incogni and Ironwall. Optery will continue operating independently with its own team, product, and technology.
- **Government sector partnership**: On September 23, 2026, Optery and Carahsoft Technology Corp. announced a strategic partnership to bring personal data removal services to the US public sector.
- **CNET Best Overall 2026**: CNET named Optery Editors' Choice and Best Overall Data Removal Service of 2026; PCMag has awarded Optery Editors' Choice for five consecutive years.
- **Fortress Cybersecurity Award**: Won 2026 Fortress Cybersecurity Award in Privacy Enhancing Technologies for its enterprise personal data removal platform.

## Incogni
- Now part of a Surfshark Group that also owns Optery and Ironwall — a significant consolidation of the market under one parent company.
- Comparison review published September 18, 2026 (CyberInsider) identified Incogni as delivering best overall value for automated recurring removals; covers 420+ brokers starting at $7.99/month.
- Review updated September 17, 2026 with refreshed pricing and coverage details.

## Privacy Bee
- **PCMag perfect 5-star rating** (August 6, 2026): First and only data removal service to earn a perfect 5/5 from PCMag, overtaking Optery and Incogni (both at 4.5 stars) as PCMag's top recommendation.
- **Acquired EraseMe.app** (August 2026): Privacy Bee acquired EraseMe.app, incorporating its technology to strengthen the platform and migrating EraseMe customers to Privacy Bee.
- Now covers **1,128 data brokers** and 185,000+ supported custom sites — broadest broker coverage of any reviewed service.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- **California DELETE Act (DROP) live**: California's Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform (DROP) became active August 1, 2026, requiring registered data brokers to check the platform every 45 days and process deletion requests — a structural tailwind for opt-out services.
- **State legislation expanding**: Connecticut enacted its own data broker registration and centralized deletion framework; New Jersey advancing an aggressive data broker regime; Vermont updating its existing law.
- **Surfshark consolidation**: With Optery now under Surfshark Group alongside Incogni and Ironwall, the market is rapidly consolidating around well-funded VPN/privacy incumbents.

## Market Summary
The week's dominant story is Surfshark's acquisition of Optery, which — combined with its existing ownership of Incogni — makes Surfshark Group the largest consolidated privacy-services platform in the data-broker removal space; meanwhile, Privacy Bee is capitalizing on its new PCMag perfect rating and EraseMe.app acquisition to aggressively position itself as the independent premium alternative. Regulatory momentum (California's DROP platform, new state laws) continues to validate and expand the market for all players.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- **California DROP — Enforcement Phase Active:** Data broker deletion obligations began August 1, 2026. 600+ registered data brokers must now process DROP deletion requests every 45 days or face $200/day per-consumer fines (doubled by SB 361). Over 300,000 Californians had submitted requests as of spring 2026 — meaningful uptake.
- **Data Broker Strike Force:** CalPrivacy's Strike Force (launched November 2025) has recorded 8+ enforcement actions to date; enforcement pressure continues to reduce re-listing rates, strengthening the long-term value proposition for automated removal services.
- **Coblentz Law / TrustArc guidance** published September 2026 clarifying DROP compliance obligations for data brokers — a signal that broker compliance is improving, which benefits removal service completion rates.

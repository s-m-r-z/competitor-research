# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-03

## DeleteMe
- California DROP 90-day compliance window closes around November 1, 2026 — brokers registered with California's DROP platform must have processed all deletion requests submitted since August 1, making this a key milestone for the regulatory landscape DeleteMe operates in.
- International plan enrollment remains closed; the company's international page shows no available countries, limiting its addressable market outside the US.
- Platform redesign shipped in May 2026: listings now marked "Removed" only after verified deletion, improving reporting accuracy.
- Pricing holds steady at ~$129/year for one person; coverage claims range from 750–976 data brokers depending on the source (some counts bundle custom-request-only and international entries).
- No product announcements or pricing changes detected in the last 24–48 hours.

## Privacy Bee
- PCMag awarded Privacy Bee a perfect 5.0-star rating and Editors' Choice in August 2026 — the first service in the category to receive that score.
- Acquired EraseMe.app (August 12, 2026), absorbing its technology and redirecting its users to Privacy Bee; self-reported broker coverage now stands at 1,128–1,229 data brokers plus 185,000+ custom sites.
- No new announcements detected in the last 24–48 hours; the August milestones remain the most recent material events.

## Incogni
- No announcements in the last 24–48 hours.
- Pricing as of early October 2026: ~$95.88/year (Standard plan), making it the lowest-priced major automated service.
- Coverage: 420+ automated data brokers, independently verified by a Deloitte audit — a credibility differentiator vs. self-reported competitor counts.
- Parent company Surfshark acquired a majority stake in Optery in September 2026 (see Optery below), expanding Surfshark's data-removal portfolio.

## Optery
- Surfshark (Incogni's parent) acquired a majority stake in Optery in September 2026; Optery continues under its own team, product, and technology.
- This creates an interesting dynamic: Surfshark now owns both Incogni and Optery — two competing services that may eventually be positioned at different market segments.
- Pricing: DIY tier at $3.99/month; full automation at $24.99/month — broadest pricing ladder in the category.
- In Consumer Reports' 2024 independent test, Optery Ultimate scored highest at 68% removal rate among paid services.
- No new announcements in the last 24–48 hours.

## Kanary
- No announcements in the last 24–48 hours.
- PCMag score of 4.0 (vs. Privacy Bee's 5.0), per August 2026 ratings.
- Covers 300–350+ data brokers; claims 90–100% removal by the 90-day mark for typical profiles.
- Pricing: ~$10.99/month or ~$99/year; US-only (no international availability).
- No significant coverage expansion or pricing changes noted recently.

## OneRep

- Multiple third-party sources continue to flag a conflict-of-interest concern related to leadership's past ties to data brokers; no company response found.
- Covers 214 data brokers per its own blog; private-broker coverage limited to Pro-tier plans.

## Removaly

## Market Summary
No major product launches or pricing changes were detected in the last 24–48 hours; the most significant recent developments are the Surfshark/Optery acquisition (September 2026) and Privacy Bee's EraseMe.app acquisition (August 2026), pointing to accelerating consolidation in the space. The California DROP 90-day compliance deadline (~November 1, 2026) is a regulatory milestone that could shift how services differentiate — paid services will increasingly need to emphasize continuous re-monitoring and non-California broker coverage to justify subscription costs alongside the free state platform.

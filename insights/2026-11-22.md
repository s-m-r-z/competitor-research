## Regulatory / Market Context
- **Data broker SEO hiding:** A U.S. Senate Joint Economic Committee (Feb 2026) report found multiple data brokers — including Comscore, IQVIA Digital, Telesign, and 6sense — placed robots.txt directives blocking search engines from indexing their opt-out pages. All have since removed the blocks. 6sense disputed the characterization. The report estimated $20B+ in consumer costs from four major broker breaches.
- **California DROP system**: 500,000+ consumers have submitted requests covering ~3 million identifiers across 680 registered brokers as of November 1, 2026. First enforcement action (LocateSmarter, $116,490 fine) set precedent for friction in opt-out flows. Additional audit rulemaking public comment period open through Jan 6, 2027.
- **Faster deletion deadlines:** AB 883 would codify 30-day deletion (down from 45 days); CalPrivacy board 5-0 vote on Nov 6 initiated formal opt-out signal rulemaking.

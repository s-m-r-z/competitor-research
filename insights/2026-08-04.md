# Competitor Insights — 2026-08-04

## Key Event: California DROP Enforcement Begins August 1, 2026

This week's most significant industry development: California's Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform (DROP) hit its enforcement deadline on **August 1, 2026**. Data brokers registered in California are now legally required to check DROP at least once every 45 days, process all pending deletion requests within 90 days, and maintain suppression lists to prevent data re-collection. As of February 2026, DROP had over 215,000 registered California users. This regulatory shift has major implications for all opt-out service competitors — the value proposition of paid services may face scrutiny as free state-level tools expand.

## DeleteMe

- Current pricing: $129/year (individual, ~$10.75/mo), $180/year (Premium), $229/year (couples), $329/year (family of 4) — no monthly billing option available
- Premium plan includes bi-monthly reports, 60 custom removal requests/year (vs. 40 standard), and 15 additional broker sites
- Covers ~750 data brokers with quarterly privacy reports and removal screenshots
- Notable gap: no dark web monitoring at any tier — a meaningful competitive weakness in 2026
- No free tier and no money-back guarantee
- Industry leading claim: 100 million personal listings removed since 2010
- Recognized as top-rated in expert rankings alongside Incogni and Aura

## Incogni (Surfshark)

- Current pricing: Standard Monthly $15.98, Standard Annual $7.99/mo, Unlimited Annual $14.99/mo, Family plans from $15.99/mo (4 members)
- Newly launched **Protect plan**: bundles data removal with NordProtect identity theft protection — includes credit monitoring, dark web monitoring, and identity remediation insurance; US-only currently
- **Unlimited plan** extends coverage to 2,000+ additional sites with human-assisted custom removals
- Unique feature: profile suppression prevents future profile creation by data brokers
- Has undergone Deloitte Independent Limited Assurance assessment confirming removal processes work as claimed — strong trust differentiator
- Covers 420+ data brokers with re-removal every 60–90 days

## Privacy Bee

- Aggressive removal approach: uses legal threats, regulatory complaints, and multi-channel pressure when brokers ignore standard opt-out requests
- Features: Google search result removal, house/map blur, tracker-blocking browser extension, private online search, Gmail inbox scan
- Wide data broker coverage; considered highly effective but criticized for excessive pricing
- Limited global availability remains a drawback for international market expansion

## Kanary

- Reported removal success rate exceeding 90% within 14 days — strong performance metric
- Covers 300+ data brokers with continuous monitoring and robust security features
- Positioned as top choice for US users prioritizing automation and rapid results
- Comparison reviews pit Kanary directly against Incogni as primary competing alternatives

## Optery

- Added **Google and Apple Maps image blurring** feature on March 5, 2026 — expanding beyond data broker removal into visual privacy
- Broad coverage of 300+ data brokers and sites
- Patented search technology combined with automation, AI, and expert manual oversight
- Strong free tier backed by flexible paid plans — competitive differentiator vs. DeleteMe
- Offers automated and human-assisted removals plus search engine coverage

## OneRep

- Automates scanning of 318 data broker sites and handles all opt-out requests
- Ongoing trust concerns: the CEO founded multiple people-search sites — the same sites the service removes users from; this narrative continues to appear in independent reviews
- Does not remove data from private-database brokers
- Only serves US residents; no international expansion noted
- Competitors (including Optery) actively publishing "Is OneRep Legit?" content to capture trust-based search traffic

## Market Summary

The August 1, 2026 enforcement of California's DROP platform is the defining industry event this week, creating a free government-backed alternative to paid services and raising the bar for what competitors must offer to justify subscription pricing. In response, market leaders are differentiating through bundled identity protection (Incogni's Protect plan), aggressive removal tactics (Privacy Bee), AI-augmented coverage (Optery), and trust certifications (Incogni's Deloitte assurance) — while the industry as a whole, valued at over $1 billion and growing at ~30% annually, consolidates around automation, breadth, and transparent reporting.

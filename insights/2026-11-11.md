## Emerging / Noteworthy
- **EasyOptOuts** continues to surface as a budget option at $19.99/year, cited in affordability comparisons.
- **California DROP milestone:** The state's DELETE Act platform now has ~590,000 enrolled Californians, delivering an average of ~200 broker deletions per user. All registered brokers (~680) must now process deletion requests and repeat every 45 days. This regulatory backdrop benefits all opt-out services by normalizing the category.
- **CalPrivacy enforcement:** Earlier 2026 actions fined LocateSmarter LLC $116,490 for failing to register under the Delete Act — signaling continued regulatory pressure on data brokers.
- **Industry trend:** Continuous re-removal (data reappears every 3–6 months) is the main value proposition emphasized across all services this cycle, alongside dark web monitoring add-ons and family plans covering up to 5 members.

## Market Summary
The 24–48 hour news cycle is dominated by DeleteMe's federal-breach PR push (Nov 10) and Incogni's early Black Friday promotion, while the broader story remains California's DROP platform demonstrating real scale at 590,000 users. Awards season rankings continue to favor Optery (CNET Best Overall) and Incogni (All About Cookies Best Overall), intensifying the positioning battle at the top of the market.

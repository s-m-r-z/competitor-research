# Competitor Insights — 2026-10-05

## Optery
- **Major Acquisition (Sept 7–8, 2026):** Surfshark acquired a majority stake in Optery, adding it as a fifth brand in Surfshark's cybersecurity ecosystem alongside Incogni, Ironwall, and HeyPolo. Optery will remain operationally independent with its own team, customers, and technical architecture.
- Ranked No. 177 on the 2026 Inc. 5000 list of America's fastest-growing private companies (first appearance); No. 33 in California, with 1,906% three-year revenue growth.
- New AI-generated detailed reports feature launched via an OpenAI partnership (note: involves sharing data with OpenAI, so privacy-conscious users may want to opt out of this feature).
- Pricing: Free–$24.99/month (4 plans: Free Basic, Core $3.99, Extended $14.99, Ultimate $24.99).
- Claims to find 40–50 more exposed broker profiles per person than competitors, and provides before-and-after screenshots.

## Incogni (Surfshark Group)
- Received third-party verification from Deloitte of its entire data removal process — a significant trust/credibility differentiator.
- Now covers 420+ data brokers with recurring removal requests every 60–90 days; reports handling 245M+ total requests.
- "Incogni Unlimited" plan adds 2,000+ custom site removal requests on top of standard coverage.
- Pricing: $7.99/month (annual) or $15.98/month (monthly); Unlimited plan adds ~$7/month.
- Some user complaints on Trustpilot about stale "in progress" removals — some not updated for 6+ months.

- Covers ~100 standard brokers; up to 750+ on higher tiers.
- Includes up to 40 custom removal requests per year on standard plan.
- No major product announcements detected in the last 24–48 hours.

## Regulatory / Market
- **California AB 883 (signed Sept 27, 2026):** Amends the Delete Act to shorten the DROP (Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform) cycle from 45 days to 30 days. Takes effect January 1, 2027 — will increase compliance pressure on data brokers and likely drive more consumers toward automated opt-out services.
- **California DROP platform** (live since August 1, 2026) now covers 543+ registered data brokers via a single consumer request interface, potentially reducing some demand for paid services among California residents who go DIY.
- CPPA enforcement is actively targeting data brokers that obscure identity or make opt-outs difficult (operating under trade names, multiple sites, parent entity loopholes).
- The data broker industry is estimated at $300B+ globally.

## Market Summary
The biggest story of the week is the Surfshark/Optery deal completing a privacy services "super-group" — Surfshark now controls both Incogni and Optery, two of the top-rated removal services, creating a dominant consolidated competitor with significant scale advantages. The upcoming California DROP 30-day cycle tightening (Jan 2027) represents a regulatory tailwind for the entire industry, likely increasing consumer awareness and urgency around data broker opt-out services.

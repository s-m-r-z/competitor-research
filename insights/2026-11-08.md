# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-08

## DeleteMe
- Pricing remains unchanged: $129/yr individual, $229/yr couple, $329/yr family (4 people); confirmed as of Oct 3, 2026
- Two-year plan available at ~$8.71/month ($209/2 yrs), no monthly billing option on any plan
- Reached milestone of 10 million consumer opt-outs (per PR Newswire)
- California's DROP platform (launched Jan 1, 2026, mandatory 90-day deletion deadline from Aug 1) is reducing DeleteMe's value proposition for CA residents specifically; DeleteMe is now positioning harder toward non-California users
- Broker coverage claims vary widely (100–960+ across sources); independent audits suggest ~100 US-focused brokers on standard plans
- Human review still a differentiator for brokers requiring fax or notarized documents

## Privacy Bee
- No new announcements detected in the last 24–48 hours
- Last notable coverage: TechRadar comparison (Nov 2025) placing top tier at ~$197/yr
- Claims 400–1,000 broker coverage; independent audits suggest 200–400 actual removals

## Kanary
- No news detected in the last 24–48 hours
- 2026 reviews (Security.org, TechRadar) continue to cite 300+ broker coverage
- Has a government/public sector channel via Carahsoft reseller partnership — a differentiator from consumer-only peers
- Kanary V2 product line documented on their blog; no new feature announcements found

## Removaly
- No longer operating; was acquired and taken offline (c. 2023); effectively dead competitor

## OneRep
- No new product or pricing announcements detected
- 2024 controversy over founder's ties to people-search sites (reported by Krebs on Security) remains a reputational overhang; unresolved per multiple 2026 competitor comparisons
- Coverage: ~310+ broker sites, monthly rescans; custom removals limited to Pro tier

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- No new feature announcements in the last 24–48 hours
- Now part of Surfshark's expanded privacy portfolio alongside Optery (see below), Ironwall, and HeyPolo
- Coverage: 420+ brokers; Deloitte-verified automated removal
- Pricing: ~$7.99/month billed annually (confirmed March 2026); Standard plan covers 207 broker sites per one comparison
- Cancellation ends re-scans — a known weakness competitors highlight

## Optery
- **Major recent news:** Surfshark announced acquisition of a majority stake in Optery in September 2026 — the most significant M&A event in the sector this year
- Both Incogni and Optery will operate as separate brands with separate teams, client bases, and technical architectures
- For existing Optery customers, no immediate operational changes
- Optery's differentiator remains screenshot proof-of-removal and more user control over individual broker requests
- Core plan ~$8.99/month; coverage ~370+ brokers (370+ per Security Hero, 200+ per other sources)
- Surfshark now controls the two most visible automated removal services — a consolidation risk for the market

## CyberForget (Emerging Competitor)
- New entrant actively marketing in 2026; self-describes as handling full opt-out cycle with automatic re-submission on re-listing
- Pricing: $19.99/month or $99/year (Personal), $199/year (Family) — per self-published comparison; no independent verification
- Coverage claims: 200–400+ brokers (AlternativeTo lists 400+; self-published post says 200+ "growing weekly")
- Published a competitor comparison on dev.to under its own org name — aggressive SEO/content play
- No independent performance testing found; treat coverage and re-listing claims as unverified

## Industry & Regulatory
- **California DROP platform** (Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform) live since Jan 1, 2026; mandatory 90-day broker deletion window effective Aug 1, 2026 — covering 543+ registered brokers with a single request; directly competes with paid services for CA residents
- **Congressional scrutiny:** JEC report (Feb 2026) pressured brokers on hidden opt-out pages; four companies confirmed to have de-indexed opt-out pages from search engines, later removed the blocking code (Fox News, March 2026)
- **Data re-listing:** Industry-wide problem — brokers re-acquire and re-list data every 3–6 months; fuels subscription model across all players

## Market Summary
The biggest story in the last 60 days is Surfshark's majority acquisition of Optery (September 2026), giving one company control over both Incogni and Optery — the two leading automated removal services — which signals consolidation and may pressure mid-tier players like DeleteMe and Kanary on pricing. California's DROP platform continues to erode the total addressable market for CA residents, pushing competitors to emphasize non-California value and B2B/enterprise channels.

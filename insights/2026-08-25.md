## Regulatory Environment
- **California DROP (Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform) enforcement began August 1, 2026** — the most significant regulatory development in the space this week. 500+ registered data brokers must now access DROP at least every 45 days to process deletion requests. Over 300,000 consumers have signed up since January 2026.
- The CPPA is advancing formal rulemaking on opt-out preference signals (Global Privacy Control) and new audit requirements under the Delete Act. Daily fines for non-compliant brokers have doubled to $200 per consumer per day.
- New Jersey, Connecticut, and Vermont are all advancing their own data broker registration and deletion frameworks, signaling a wave of state-level regulation beyond California.

## Market Summary
The biggest story this week is **Privacy Bee's PCMag 5-star win** and the **California DROP enforcement launch** on August 1 — both are reshaping competitive positioning, with services now competing on broker coverage depth and third-party credibility. Regulatory tailwinds are accelerating consumer awareness industry-wide, benefiting all major players while creating new compliance overhead for the data brokers themselves.

## Regulatory / Market
- **California DROP — November 2026 milestone:** Per the CPPA timeline, November 2026 is the checkpoint by which all registered data brokers should have completed their full initial download-and-delete cycle (45-day cadence, effective August 1, 2026). This is a structural tailwind for all opt-out services as manual compliance increases broker friction.
- **Federal legislation:** SECURE Data Act and Online Privacy Act of 2026 remain in early committee stages — no federal privacy law yet.
- **Incogni's 245M+ removal request milestone** (Deloitte, July 2025) continues to be a widely cited credibility anchor in industry comparisons.

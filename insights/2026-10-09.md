## Incogni (Surfshark)
- **Surfshark achieves full post-quantum WireGuard security (Oct 7–8, 2026):** Parent company Surfshark announced the integration of ML-DSA authentication alongside ML-KEM key exchange into WireGuard, claiming a "world first" for full post-quantum VPN security. The update is currently live on iOS, macOS, and Windows, with additional platforms coming. Sources: TechRadar (Oct 7), The Quantum Insider (Oct 8), Surfshark release notes.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- **California DROP enforcement fully operational:** The Delete Request and Opt-out Platform's first 45-day processing window closed ~September 15, 2026 (per yesterday's file). Brokers operating in California now face legal obligation to honor deletion requests via this free state tool, covering 543+ registered brokers. Paid services continue to position around non-California coverage and ongoing monitoring as their primary value-add.
- **JEC report on opt-out obstacles (May 2026):** A Senate Joint Economic Committee minority report documented how some brokers — including Findem — made opt-out pages easier to find only after regulatory pressure, underscoring that self-service opt-out remains unreliable and supporting the paid-service value proposition.
- **Consumer Reports benchmark still active reference point:** Optery top-tier at 68% removal, EasyOptOuts at 65% ($19.99/year flat), DeleteMe as a mid-tier performer. These figures continue to circulate in comparison content and are shaping buying decisions.

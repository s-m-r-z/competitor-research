## Regulatory: California DROP — Day 18 of Enforcement
- August 1, 2026 enforcement deadline has passed; brokers now face $200/request/day penalties for non-compliance with the 45-day DELETE cycle.
- Law firms (Alston & Bird, TrustArc, Ketch, Transcend) continue publishing compliance guidance — indicating sustained demand for DROP interpretation among corporate clients.
- 300,000+ California consumers signed up for DROP since its January 1 launch. The free government tool is a structural headwind for paid services focused narrowly on California-registered brokers.
- **Market implication:** Services that differentiate on non-California broker coverage (Privacy Bee's 185K+ custom sites, OneRep Pro's 877 sites), removal speed, or human oversight retain value even as DROP absorbs the California-only use case.

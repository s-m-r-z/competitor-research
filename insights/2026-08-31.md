# Competitor Insights — 2026-08-31

## Regulatory Environment — California DROP Platform
- California's DELETE Act **DROP (Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform)** went fully live August 1, 2026, with deletion obligations now enforced
- Data brokers must log into DROP every 45 days, download hashed consumer identifiers, and delete matching records — penalties up to **$200/day** for non-compliance
- DROP allows California residents to submit a single deletion request to 600+ registered data brokers simultaneously, at no cost
- This is the most significant regulatory shift in the space this year; services that automate DROP-aware workflows have a new competitive differentiator
- Source: [Privacy Rights Clearinghouse](https://privacyrights.org/resources-tools/advocacy/deletion-obligations-under-drop-are-here-data-brokers-must-now-delete), [Alston & Bird](https://www.alstonprivacy.com/drop-is-coming-due-what-californias-delete-act-means-for-data-brokers-in-august/)

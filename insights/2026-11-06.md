## Regulatory & Market Context
- California's DROP (Data Removal On-ramp Program) hit its first 90-day milestone: ~590,000 Californians enrolled, averaging ~200 broker deletions each; 680+ brokers registered. This creates a free DIY alternative for California residents that could reduce paid-service conversion.
- CFPB data broker rule withdrawn in May 2025 — federal regulatory tailwind has dissipated, leaving state-level law (California, Texas, etc.) as the primary pressure on brokers.
- Security Boulevard published a November 2026 "Secure Your Digital Life" checklist recommending data removal services alongside other hygiene steps — signals continued mainstream consumer awareness.

## Market Summary
The Surfshark/Incogni acquisition of Optery is the defining M&A story shaping the competitive landscape, consolidating two top-reviewed services under one parent while they remain independent brands. California's DROP milestone is an emerging headwind for paid services targeting California consumers, as free government-facilitated removal becomes more visible and credible.

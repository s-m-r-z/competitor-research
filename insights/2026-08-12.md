This is the primary inbound driver for Optery in August — being cited in nearly every "best of" roundup.

- Coverage: 300–645+ data brokers/sites depending on plan tier; Free / Core $39/yr / Extended $149/yr / Ultimate $249/yr pricing unchanged.

## Incogni
- **55% discount active in August 2026**: Incogni is running an aggressive summer promotion — 55% off annual plans with code IncogniCNL, dropping the effective cost to approximately $7.49/month. This is the most aggressive discount in the market right now and will drive top-of-funnel volume from deal-comparison sites.
- **Head-to-head comparison articles proliferating**: Multiple high-DA outlets (CyberInsider, Incogni's own blog, OneRep's blog, SecurityHero) published "Incogni vs DeleteMe" comparison articles in August 2026. Incogni wins on price; DeleteMe wins on coverage breadth (750+ sites vs. ~420).

- Coverage: 420+ brokers; pricing at $89.88/year (individual annual).

## DeleteMe
- **Consumer Reports efficacy gap exposed**: DeleteMe rated as a "mid-level performer" in Consumer Reports testing alongside IDX and Kanary, at ~27% removal rate versus Optery's 68%. This gap is being widely cited in 2026 comparison articles and may suppress conversion for premium-conscious buyers.
- **Coverage breadth still strongest**: DeleteMe covers 750+ data broker sites — more than any other major service — with custom removal requests included at the base tier ($129/year). This is the primary counter-narrative to efficacy critiques.
- **Value-add differentiators**: Email masking included at standard plan; phone number masking available at $7/number. No other major removal service bundles this.
- No new product announcements or pricing changes in the last 48 hours. Re-check cadence remains quarterly (every 90 days).

## Regulatory: California DROP — Day 11
- DROP enforcement remains in effect since August 1, 2026. 600+ registered data brokers must process deletion requests within 45 days of receipt, checking DROP at minimum every 45 days.
- Fine structure: $200/day per unprocessed request (SB 361 doubled this from $100).
- Market impact: Services are responding by emphasizing non-California broker coverage, speed, and proactive monitoring as reasons to subscribe even for California residents who now have free DROP access.
- State-level momentum accelerating: New Jersey, Connecticut, and Vermont all advancing data broker frameworks in parallel.

## Market Summary
The competitive dynamic on August 12 is sharpest around efficacy perception: Optery's consumer-facing award sweep and Consumer Reports leadership (68% removal) are widening its positioning gap over DeleteMe (mid-level, ~27%) and Kanary, while Incogni's active 55% summer discount is the most aggressive short-term conversion play in the market. California DROP's enforcement — now 11 days in — continues to reshape value-proposition narratives across the entire category, with services pivoting messaging toward speed, non-CA coverage, and proactive monitoring to justify paid subscriptions alongside the state's free opt-out platform.

# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-01

## DeleteMe
- International plan enrollment has closed; US plans remain available at $129–$329/year.
- Claims 750+ broker coverage, though third-party counts cite up to 969 brokers — discrepancy unresolved.
- California's DROP platform (launched Jan 1, 2026) is now past its August 1 broker-compliance deadline; DeleteMe has blogged about it as a consumer resource.
- Independent Consumer Reports testing (2024) rated DeleteMe at ~27% removal effectiveness, placing it sixth of eight services — well below Optery Ultimate (68%) and EasyOptOuts (65%).

## Incogni
- Ran a Cybersecurity Awareness Week promotional discount (up to 58% off annual plans) in late October 2026 — campaign appearing across tech media.
- Deloitte completed an assurance audit (effective July 18, 2025, published early October 2026) confirming coverage of 420+ data brokers and process integrity.
- Pricing holds at ~$7.99/month billed annually; 30-day money-back guarantee.
- Coverage figure on Unlimited plan is cited as 3,000+ sites by some sources — treat with caution as it comes from a single, less-established source.
- Has processed 245 million+ removal requests per the Deloitte-commissioned assurance report.

## Privacy Bee
- PCMag awarded Privacy Bee a perfect 5-out-of-5 rating and Editors' Choice on August 5, 2026 — the first perfect PCMag score in data-removal history.
- Acquired EraseMe.app in August 2026 to expand data-removal technology; EraseMe.app customers are being transitioned to Privacy Bee; financial terms undisclosed.
- SOC 2 Type II certified, ISO 27001 compliant, BBB A+ rated.
- Pricing starts at $96/year; enterprise/multi-license tiers at $399–$699/year.
- Coverage extends beyond standard people-search sites into insurance, marketing, healthcare data broker, and financial aggregator categories.

## Kanary
- Shifted product strategy: launched "Kanary Copilot" as a mobile app, though the web version remains for existing users.
- Coverage: 300+ brokers with continuous monitoring; extends to major tech platforms and search engines.
- Pricing tiers: free Community plan, $9.99/month Professional, $50/month Advanced — but sources conflict on plan limits; verify directly.
- 30-day money-back guarantee; setup time under five minutes.
- Noted weakness: no identity theft insurance bundled; no global (non-US) coverage.

## Optery
- CNET reportedly named Optery its 2026 Editors' Choice and Best Overall Data Removal Service (vendor claim — not independently verified here).
- Consumer Reports ranked Optery #1 for removal effectiveness in recent testing (vendor claim).
- Tiered coverage: ~90 sites (Core), ~240 (Extended), ~370 (Ultimate); Ultimate priced at ~$14.99/month.
- User complaints noted: slow removal progress and aggressive upselling reported in some reviews; occasional false "removed" status.

## OneRep
- Pricing remains ~$8.33/month billed annually — cheaper than Optery's mid-tier.
- Coverage cited as 319 sites (standard) and 878 (Pro); focuses on public data broker sites, not private brokers.
- Ongoing conflict-of-interest concern flagged by multiple reviewers relating to ownership history in the data broker industry.
- Rescans sites to verify removals — a differentiator noted positively by some reviewers.

## Removaly
- No significant new announcements or coverage found in the last 48 hours.

## Market Summary
California's DROP platform hit its first major compliance deadline on October 30, 2026, forcing 600+ registered data brokers to process deletion requests — a regulatory tailwind for the entire opt-out services industry. The competitive landscape is consolidating around effectiveness claims: Privacy Bee's perfect PCMag score and acquisition of EraseMe.app, Incogni's Deloitte-validated audit, and Optery's reported CNET award suggest top players are leaning heavily into third-party credibility signals to differentiate as the market matures.

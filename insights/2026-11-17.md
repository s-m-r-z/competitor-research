- Also published Nov 10 post on Meta's Muse Image feature and AI data inferences from phones (co-built Priva-See demo tool, 465 testers).
- Continuing to position California's DROP platform as *complementary* to DeleteMe rather than a replacement, emphasizing DROP covers ~200 out of 750+ brokers they claim to remove from.

## Regulatory / Market Context
- **California DROP ongoing enforcement**: ~450,000 deletion requests submitted to date; ~200 brokers complying per request on average. Enforcement penalties of $200/request/day in effect since August 2026.
- Black Friday/Cyber Monday season is driving multi-service promotional activity — Cloaked also confirmed at up to 67% off (CyberInsider roundup).
- **Surfshark Group consolidation**: Now controls Incogni, Optery, Ironwall, and HeyPolo — four of the most-cited services in the removal space. Competitor differentiation increasingly depends on pricing, coverage breadth, and non-US market reach.
- Data broker economy projected to reach $561B by 2029 (Cloaked research), underscoring long-term market growth for removal services.

## Market Summary
Incogni's early Black Friday sale (58% off, live now) is the most significant current competitive event, potentially pulling price-sensitive customers away from rivals ahead of the holiday promotional window; Privacy Bee's August PCMag 5-star rating and EraseMe.app acquisition are still amplifying its positioning in comparison content. Surfshark Group's growing portfolio (Incogni + Optery) is consolidating market authority, while emerging entrant CyberForget is mounting a content-marketing challenge to established players.

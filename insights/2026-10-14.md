## Regulatory & Market Developments (October 2026)
- **California "Delete Week"**: On October 12, 2026, the California Privacy Protection Agency (CalPrivacy) launched its first-ever "Delete Week" to raise consumer awareness of data broker deletion rights under the DELETE Act — directly beneficial for all opt-out service marketing
- **DROP platform enforcement**: Since August 1, 2026, data brokers must retrieve deletion requests from California's DELETE Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP) every 45 days; penalties are $200/request/day for non-compliance — increases competitive value of automated opt-out services
- **Request volume**: California filings show 58M+ deletion requests submitted in 2024, with ~890K denied — illustrates scale of unmet demand
- **Upcoming changes**: Increased broker registration fees in 2027; mandatory third-party audits starting January 2028; California's Opt Me Out Act (AB 566) requires browsers to offer Global Privacy Control (GPC) functionality from January 1, 2027

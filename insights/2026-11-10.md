# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-10

## DeleteMe
- Closed enrollment on international plans; US plan remains at $129/year for 1 person, $229/year for 2, $329/year for a family.
- Broker coverage now listed at 969 on its own site (some guides still cite 750+, depending on source/date).
- All About Cookies 2026 awards gave DeleteMe its **Best for Couples** category; Wirecutter still rates it the best dedicated service, but The Optout Project's 2026 test ranked Aura above it for most users.
- No specific product announcement found in the last 24–48 hours.

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- CostBench reports a **new fifth pricing plan added September 5, 2026** (unconfirmed on Incogni's own site; verify at incogni.com/pricing).
- Current pricing: ~$7.99/mo (annual) / $15.98/mo (monthly). "Unlimited" plan is annual-only and adds custom removal requests beyond the standard broker list.
- Covers 420+ data brokers; verification claims independently audited by Deloitte.
- **NordVPN bundle** launched March 2026 — Incogni available as an add-on within NordVPN subscriptions, expanding distribution reach.
- Stork.ai mentions an **Incogni Protect Plan** offering up to $1M identity theft/cyber extortion insurance — not yet confirmed on an official page.
- No new announcements found specifically in the last 24–48 hours.

## OneRep
- Pricing: Individual at ~$8.33–$14.95/mo, depending on tier and billing cycle; family plans at $180–$359.40/year.
- Covers 310+ data brokers; SOC 2 certified as of 2026.
- Multiple reviews continue to flag **concerns about OneRep's historical data-handling practices**, potentially impacting consumer trust.
- No new announcements found in the last 24–48 hours.

## Optery
- Core plan at $3.99/mo ($39/yr); Extended at $149/yr; Ultimate at $249/yr; free basic tier available.
- Differentiator: auto-generated removal links with **verification screenshots** of removed profiles.
- Independent 30-day study (2025, 71 participants) placed Optery in the mid-range; study did not include Privacy Bee or Removaly.
- No new announcements found in the last 24–48 hours.

## Privacy Bee
- Plans: $96, $197, and $799/year. Claims removal from 18,000+ corporate databases and 1,319 data brokers.
- Differentiator: escalates ignored opt-outs with **legal demand letters**, though some reviews note this can slow removals.
- No controlled independent study found confirming removal rates.
- No new announcements found in the last 24–48 hours.

## Kanary
- Positioned around people-search sites and high-risk users (doxxing, stalking).
- 2025 peer-reviewed study (71 participants, 30 days) found Kanary had the **lowest removal rate at 23.4%**, vs. Incogni at 76.6%.
- Higher pricing tier than most competitors; niche positioning may be strategic for a narrow market segment.
- No new announcements found in the last 24–48 hours.

## Removaly
- Very limited independent coverage; claims removal from Google and 199 data brokers.
- No recent news, reviews, or product announcements found.

## Emerging / Market Context
- **California DROP system milestone (Nov 5, 2026):** California's privacy regulator reports ~590,000 Californians enrolled in DROP; 680+ registered brokers must now process deletions every 45 days (compliance started Aug 1, 2026). Average user has seen ~200 brokers comply since launch. This is the most significant recent regulatory development affecting the whole industry.
- **Federal legislation stalled:** The SECURE Data Act and Online Privacy Act of 2026 remain in early committee stages — no near-term federal preemption of state frameworks expected.
- **Aura** emerging as a top-ranked alternative in independent 2026 tests (The Optout Project), signaling growing competitive pressure on traditional dedicated services like DeleteMe.

## Market Summary
No single competitor made a major product announcement in the last 24–48 hours; the dominant story is California's DROP compliance milestone, which now gives 590,000 consumers a government-backed removal channel and sets a new baseline all paid services must compete against. Incogni's distribution expansion via NordVPN and its new plan tier represent the most recent competitive moves, while DeleteMe faces mounting pressure from Aura in head-to-head 2026 reviews.

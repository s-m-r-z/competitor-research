## Industry / Regulatory
- **California DROP now fully enforced:** California's Delete Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP), launched in early 2026 under the Delete Act, entered mandatory enforcement on August 1, 2026. Registered data brokers must check DROP every 45 days and delete matching records within 45 days. Covers 543+ registered brokers in the state — a significant structural advantage for California residents using DIY opt-out over paid services.
- **Hidden opt-out pages under scrutiny:** A May 2026 Joint Economic Committee report found that data broker Findem had used `noindex` tags on its opt-out page, hiding it from Google search. Findem removed the tag after public exposure.
- **State privacy laws expanding:** 23 states reported to now provide residents with enforceable data deletion rights (unverified count from secondary sources).

## Market Summary
No major product launches or pricing changes were detected in the last 48 hours; the most significant recent competitive move is Privacy Bee's acquisition of EraseMe.app in August 2026 and its PCMag 5-star recognition, which positions it as the coverage and credibility leader. The California DROP platform entering full enforcement represents the most structurally significant industry shift, as it provides a free DIY alternative for a major U.S. market and may pressure paid services to differentiate on national coverage and automation speed.

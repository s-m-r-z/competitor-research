# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-24

## DeleteMe
- Pricing: ~$129/year for individuals (nearly $130 per CNBC); coverage claims 750+ data brokers
- Key differentiator: human reviewers handle complex removals requiring manual forms, fax, or notarized documentation
- Potential acquisition of Block Party referenced on DeleteMe's news page; date and terms unconfirmed — monitor for official announcement

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- **Active Black Friday promotion**: Early sale live — 58% off annual plans; Standard plan reported at ~$6.79/month (~$81.50–$95.88/year billed annually); sale runs ahead of Black Friday (Nov 27) through Cyber Monday (Nov 30)
- Product updates (reported June 2026): mobile app launched, 24/7 support added, identity theft protection bundled in Protection Plan
- Competitor criticism in circulation: Aura (a rival) has published messaging claiming Incogni's "blind opt-out" approach sends requests to all brokers regardless of whether they hold a user's data — worth monitoring as a narrative that could affect consumer perception
- Parent Surfshark Group now owns Incogni, Ironwall (B2B), and Optery (see below)

## Optery
- **Acquired by Surfshark Group** (majority stake, announced ~September 7–8, 2026): operating as a separate brand with its own team, roadmap, pricing, and infrastructure
- Free tier remains available — described as "the only major service with a genuinely useful free tier"
- Not bundled with Surfshark VPN; sold separately via Optery.com
- TechRadar calls it "one of the strongest data removal services going into 2026"

## CyberForget (Emerging)
- Self-described new entrant positioning against DeleteMe, Incogni, and Optery
- Claimed pricing: $99/year (Personal), $199/year (Family); claims 200+ broker sites covered (growing weekly)
- Currently lacks independent third-party reviews; primary coverage is vendor-authored content on dev.to — treat claims with caution until verified by established tech media

## California DROP Platform (Regulatory)
- California's Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform (DROP) went live in 2026, allowing CA residents to send one deletion request to 600–665+ registered data brokers via a free state portal
- Major competitive pressure on paid services in the California market; services must now differentiate on scope (non-CA brokers), monitoring, re-removal, and ease-of-use for non-CA residents

## Market Summary
The week of Black Friday 2026 is active with early promotional pricing across data removal services — Incogni leading with a 58% early-access deal — while the most structurally significant development remains Surfshark Group's consolidation of both Incogni and Optery under one roof, reshaping competitive dynamics for the entire category. California's free DROP platform continues to pressure the value proposition of paid services for the state's ~40M residents.

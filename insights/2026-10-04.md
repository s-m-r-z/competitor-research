## Market Summary
The biggest near-term competitive threat to all paid services remains California's state-run DROP platform (Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform), which launched January 1, 2026 and has enrolled 300,000+ Californians for free — compressing the value proposition for paid services for California residents. Optery's GuidePoint Security partnership signals a broader B2B enterprise pivot as the consumer market faces pricing pressure from free government alternatives.

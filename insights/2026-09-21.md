- **CalPrivacy DROP Enforcement Advisory (ongoing)**: Nelson Mullins published analysis this week of CalPrivacy's latest DROP enforcement advisory ("FAQs and Another Clear Warning to Data Brokers") — CalPrivacy is issuing structured FAQ guidance to increase broker compliance pressure. The $200/day-per-consumer-request penalty structure is the key enforcement lever.
- **345,000+ deletion requests** have been submitted through DROP since the January 1, 2026 consumer launch — the platform is gaining traction ahead of full mandatory processing enforcement.
- **Data Broker Strike Force** (launched November 2025): CalPrivacy has now recorded 8 enforcement actions — a record; the Strike Force is actively investigating CCPA and Delete Act violations, broadening scope beyond registration failures.
- **CalPrivacy Enforcement Advisory 2026-01** (issued September 3): $200/day fine for inaccurate data broker registration information — a tightening compliance environment that reduces data broker recidivism and indirectly reduces the "re-listing" problem that removal services face.
- **Colorado AI Regulations**: Proposed August 11, 2026; comments closed September 4; public hearing October 26; proposed effective date January 1, 2027 — adjacent regulatory pressure that may impact data brokers using algorithmic profiling.
- **Surveillance pricing narrative** continues to dominate the September privacy news cycle — increasing general consumer awareness is a broad tailwind for the whole removal-service category.

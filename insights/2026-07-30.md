## California DROP — Critical Market Event (Tomorrow)
- **August 1, 2026 enforcement begins:** All 600+ registered California data brokers must start processing DROP deletion requests — the single most significant regulatory moment for this industry in 2026
- 260,000+ California consumers already enrolled; brokers face $200/consumer/day in fines for non-compliance — theoretical liability of $1.5B per missed 45-day cycle
- This is a free state-run alternative to paid removal services for California residents, but its scope is limited to California-registered brokers only; paid services maintain value for national and international coverage
- Expect consumer demand spikes for removal services as DROP drives mainstream awareness of data broker issues

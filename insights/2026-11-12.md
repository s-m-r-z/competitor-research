# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-12

## California DROP Platform (Regulatory)
- MLex (Nov 5) reports ~590,000 Californians enrolled in DROP; 680+ registered data brokers now obligated to comply
- Average user has seen ~200 brokers comply per their deletion request — a meaningful free alternative to paid services
- Brokers required to re-delete data every 45 days under the Delete Act; DROP now also functions as a Do Not Sell/Share request
- DROP's growing adoption is a continued competitive headwind for paid removal services targeting California residents

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- CostBench changelog shows a **fifth pricing plan added September 5, 2026** — plan range now $7.99–$22.99/month (Standard and Unlimited confirmed at $7.99 and $14.99/mo per Toolradar October 2026 check)
- New **Incogni Protect** tier bundles data removal with identity theft recovery insurance up to $1 million
- New **Incogni Concierge** service launched for US residents (white-glove assisted removal)
- New **privacy score** feature gives users a single numeric exposure rating
- Coverage: Deloitte-attested 420+ brokers as of July 2025; third-party sources cite 750–1,420+ (unverified)

## DeleteMe (Abine)
- Dashboard redesign rolled out to all users (reported ~September 2026): cleaner UI and new **custom data removal requests** feature
- Custom removal requests require users to wait for their first Privacy Report before submitting
- Continues to cite 100M+ successful data removals across its customer base
- Published a California DROP explainer (Oct 9) framing DROP as complementary to, not a replacement for, DeleteMe

## Optery
- **Expanded Reach** feature sends preemptive opt-outs to brokers that don't publicly confirm whether they hold your data
- **API features for developers and platform partners** launched in 2025, now a differentiator for B2B/enterprise segment
- Covers 380–625+ broker sites depending on plan tier
- Higher tiers add human-assisted opt-outs, before/after screenshots, and Google/Bing outdated-content removal

## Privacy Bee

- Recent third-party testing (2026) shows a 61% removal success rate; covers 1,000+ brokers including insurance, healthcare, and financial data aggregators — wider scope than most rivals
- No pricing changes found

## Market Summary
California's DROP platform has surpassed 590,000 enrollments and now covers 680+ brokers, increasing pressure on all paid services to demonstrate ROI beyond what the free state tool offers; meanwhile, Incogni is aggressively expanding with a new fifth pricing tier and Protect/Concierge upsell features, signaling a push to capture higher-value customers before free regulatory alternatives erode the entry-level market.

# Competitor Insights — 2026-11-28

## DeleteMe
- Running a **Black Friday sale (Nov 21 – Dec 5, 2026)**: 20% off all plans (individual, couple, family), down from the 30% promotion offered in 2025.
- Launched a new **"Gold" tier** aimed at individuals with higher public visibility or personal risk, alongside the existing "Workforce" (core) option.
- PCMag rating stands at **3.5/5** as of August 2026, notably below key competitors Privacy Bee (5.0), Optery (4.5), and Incogni (4.5).
- TechRadar is running a co-branded 30% off deal for its readers this Black Friday season.

## Privacy Bee
- Received the **first-ever perfect 5-star (Exemplary) rating from PCMag** on August 5, 2026, earning an Editors' Choice designation — the first data removal service to achieve this.
- PCMag's 2026 ranking at time of review: Privacy Bee 5.0, Optery 4.5, Incogni 4.5, Cloaked 4.0, Kanary 4.0, IDX Complete 4.0, PrivacyHawk 4.0, DeleteMe 3.5, Aura 3.5.
- Pricing starts at **$96/year**; no free tier available.
- Positioned by TechRadar as one of the strongest "set-and-forget" platforms in 2026.

## Incogni (Surfshark)
- **September 2026 pricing change**: Added a fifth plan tier, with the range now spanning **$7.99–$22.99/month**.
- Expanded feature set now includes: **Exposure Scanner** (unlimited custom domain removal), a **mobile app**, additional 24/7 support channels, and **identity theft protection** bundled in the Protection Plan tier.
- New **Incogni Concierge** service and **Incogni Protect** tier launched (Incogni Protect includes identity theft insurance).
- Now covers 420+ data broker sites with re-submission every 60–90 days.
- Parent company Surfshark Group completed acquisition of Optery in September 2026, further consolidating its data-removal portfolio (also includes HeyPolo and Ironwall).

## Optery
- **Acquired by Surfshark Group** (majority stake) in early September 2026. Optery operates as a standalone company with the same leadership and team intact; no product merger planned.
- **Black Friday deal (announced Nov 27, 2026)**: One year of Optery Core for **$29.99** (regularly $39), covering 350+ automated sites. Ultimate tier covers 620+ sites.
- Maintains PCMag rating of **4.5/5** and Editors' Choice status alongside Incogni and Privacy Bee.
- The acquisition makes Surfshark Group the dominant consolidator in automated data removal.

## Kanary

## Regulatory & Market Context
- **California DROP platform**: 300,000+ Californians enrolled as of June 2026; data brokers required to process deletion requests starting August 1, 2026. California's first enforcement action under the Delete Act was taken against LocateSmarter in mid-August 2026.
- **Industry consolidation**: Surfshark Group now controls Incogni, Optery, HeyPolo, and Ironwall — making it the leading aggregator of data-removal assets globally.
- **Black Friday pricing pressure**: Active promotions from DeleteMe (20% off) and Optery ($29.99/year) signal increasing price competition heading into year-end.

## Market Summary
The data broker opt-out space is experiencing rapid consolidation, with Surfshark Group's September 2026 acquisition of Optery cementing its dominance, while Privacy Bee's first-ever perfect PCMag score reshapes the premium positioning narrative. Black Friday promotions from DeleteMe and Optery are compressing price expectations just as California's DROP regulatory platform begins reducing the perceived value differential between paid services and free government tooling for CA residents.

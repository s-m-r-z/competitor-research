## Regulatory & Market Context
- **California DROP enforcement is live:** As of August 1, 2026, data brokers must process deletion requests from California's state-run platform every 45 days or face **$200/day per request** fines
- **First enforcement action (August 11, 2026):** CalPrivacy fined LocateSmarter **$116,490** — the first penalty under the Delete Act; Cybba and SalesIntel Research also penalized for late/failed registration
- **300,000+ Californians** have already submitted deletion requests through DROP since the January 2026 launch
- **2027 registration fee increase:** Data broker registration fees will rise to **$9,500** (up $3,500 from 2026), with CalPrivacy citing rising administrative costs

## Market Summary
The data broker opt-out industry is experiencing a significant regulatory tailwind from California's DROP platform going into full enforcement mode, which benefits all removal services by increasing consumer awareness and urgency. Privacy Bee's PCMag 5-star milestone and Incogni's Deloitte audit are the two most notable competitive moves from the last 30 days, with both services leaning into third-party validation as a key differentiator; no major pricing or product launches were detected in the immediate 48-hour window.
